RUN     The orchestrator (with debugging agents): integration and the

        is triaged: model error, executor error, or SUT bug; SUT bugs
        get bug-NN-<slug>.txt and, if easy, a fix (DRV-FIX agents,
        unit test first, image rebuilt, the run repeated). Runs in
        06-runs.txt.
REVIEW  Opus reviewers of MODEL+EXEC (after P1) and of every driver
        fix; findings in 05-review*.txt.

Prototypes
----------
P1 off-line: mbt-cxl generate --profile small --steps 40 >
   code.gen.sh, MBT_MODE=offline MBT_SCRIPT=..., in the cluster.
   Measures: steps per minute, failures, what the expectations catch.
P2 on-line lock-step: MBT_MODE=online with planning ahead off
   (--plan-ahead=false), small profile, 20 min.
P3 on-line planning ahead (default), full profile, 20 min, then 2 h:
   planning time vs step time, how often the plan was reused,
   coverage growth in the real run vs the off-line estimate.
P4 model and coverage variants off-line (MODEL), the best mix becomes
   the default; documented with numbers.
P5 verification: oracle cost per settle; a deliberate model mutation
   (e.g. predict no fencing) must be caught by the oracle at the
   first relevant settle (self-test of the oracle).
