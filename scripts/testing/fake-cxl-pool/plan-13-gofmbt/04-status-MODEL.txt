4. Prediction rules (the specification the oracle checks)
---------------------------------------------------------
Phases: absent, starting (created or promoted in this window, holds
its resource), restarting (crashed), running, pending (unschedulable,
holds nothing), attaching (allocated, waits for its pool controller),
blocked (allocated, its Prepare refused by a decoder hole, A9),
unblocking (the hole cleared, kubelet's retry pending), either (a
controller-kill race, now always resolved into alternatives), deleting.
R1 Capacity (01-findings-driver 2.1-2.3): an exclusive pool device has
   one holder cluster-wide; a shared one one exclusive holder (sexcl)
   or any number of shares; a local memdev --local-blocks blocks, one
   per lnuma/ldax/lsteer pod and per lease (128Mi = one block).
R2 create: no pod in the slot, no waiter and no unsettled transfer on
   its resource; fits now -> starting; fits once what is being
   released is gone -> starting, the resource "contested" (the only
   candidate; nothing else asks until the settle); else pending (the
   one waiter). No race whose winner is the scheduler's choice.
R3 delete: a holder's waiter is promoted (starting, contested) if it
   fits when the deleting holders are gone; likewise on a lease
   release (lease-put, requester deleted). An exclusive device moves
   to another node: detach then attach (F/controller/reconciler.go:
   316-327).
R4 settle: deleting -> absent, restarting -> running, starting ->
   running, attaching (its controller is down), blocked or unblocking
   (R15). Attachments of a pool whose controller is up: the nodes of
   its holders (also blocked ones: allocated claims are attached),
   exclusive iff a sexcl holds the shared device there (reconciler.go:
   64-82 exclusiveResult).
R5 Controller down (ctrl-kill): new pool claims wait for the attach,
   also on a node where the device is attached for another claim
   (writeStatus, reconciler.go:336, is per claim); attachments stay
   (only the controller detaches, reconciler.go:414). What was
   changing at the kill is "maybe" (attached or not) and a pod that
   was starting may have prepared or not: alternatives (R15) that the
   observation decides on-line; off-line a kill is not generated while
   a prepare of its pool is pending. ctrl-start: waiting pods prepare.
   ctrl-restart, server-restart, kubelet-restart: nothing changes.
R6 Regions (D/local.go:993 releaseClaim -> :1108 releaseRegion,
   D/pool.go:693 prepareDevice): a local region exists while it has a
   slice; numa slices kmem with 128Mi online on the hb0 node, dax
   slices device_dax; pool numa: one kmem dax device with the whole
   device online on its host bridge's node; pool dax: one device_dax.
   MemTotal of a CXL node = the numa memory prepared on it.
R7 Fencing set F per VM (D/fence.go:95 Fence, :121 seed, :154
   preparedNodes): seeded at each driver start (driver-kill,
   driver-restart, containerd-restart) with the numa claims prepared on
   the VM then (in flux -> maybe); grows at every settle and numa
   lease-get. A container of a test pod or companion without a numa
   claim or numa lease on CXL node X has no X in mems_effective when X
   is in F or has memory on the VM; a numa claimer or lease holder has
   its node. (F alone is observable only through memory on the node.)
R8 Steering (doc/usage.md:879-881, A17 = bug-04 fix): an lsteer
   container started while its driver runs has memory.high != max;
   after any driver restart (driver-kill, driver-restart, containerd-
   restart) max, fenced with its claim like an unsteered container; a
   later restart of the container is steered again; (re)started in a
   kill window: either (adopted). Every other container: max.
R9 Leases (D/broker.go:1277 requesterDone, :1297 containerGone, :1328
   containerStarted): lease-get only with a free block (blocks being
   released count), no driver kill window, and its slice's region
   available now (R15); Ready when cxl-request returns, one companion
   Running on the requester's node; kept across a requester crash;
   released with the requester pod or by lease-put.
R10 Data (doc/usage.md:1062-1078): pool dax devices keep their bytes
   between claims and nodes, unknown after a numa use; shared pool
   devices keep them; a new local dax slice reads "" (D/local.go:855
   zeroSlice); a crash keeps the content; never-written content is
   unknown: any read is accepted (and learnt on-line). write on sro
   fails.
R11 Restarts: crash, driver-kill and containerd-restart only below
   --max-restarts (2) restarts (kubelet back-off 10/20/40 s, A6);
   driver-restart: a new pod, restarts 0.
R12 Env (pkg/cxl/api/api.go:298 Env, :388 FormatSerial; D/node_state.go
   :1476, D/pool.go:178 memory): CXL_<SERIAL HEX>_MODE, SIZE (bytes),
   SERIAL, SHARED, NODE (numa) or DAX (/dev/dax*) and READONLY (dax);
   lease: CXL_LEASE_SOCKET=/run/cxl/cxl.sock; plain: none; CXL_CLAIM_*
   ignored; any other CXL_ variable is a difference.
R13 State files, CDI (D/node_state.go:1447 buildClaimsCDISpec,
   D/lease_claims.go:122 leaseCDIDevice): prepared == info; running
   local and lease claims plus one companion per held lease;
   preparedPoolClaims == running pool claims of the VM; localDevices of
   this boot, no "-" owner, one slice per claim; cxl-claims.yaml
   claim-<uid>-<n> of local and lease claims, cxl-pool.yaml of pool
   claims, cxl-leases.yaml lease-<uid> of lease claims.
R14 Teardown: settle, ctrl-start of stopped controllers, delete every
   pod (waiters first), a final settle with nothing left (no pods,
   companions, k8s attachments, regions, pool memdevs, CXL memory,
   prepared claims, CDI devices, decoders).
R15 Decoder stacks (A9, bug-02): per (VM, host bridge) the regions in
   creation order. Users of a region on a VM: the claim (exclusive
   pool device), the claims of the node (shared device), the slices of
   pods and leases (local). Region events of a window: prepare (a pod
   starting with its Prepare in the window: local, or pool with its
   controller up; at ctrl-start for those that waited; a lease-get,
   synchronous) and unprepare (delete of a prepared pod, lease
   release). A prepare of a user without its region pushes it if the
   stack has no hole, else the pod is BLOCKED; an unprepare of the
   last user destroys it (the top pops with the holes below, else a
   hole). The settle plays the events in every order the window
   allows (a lease-get before what came after it; a promoted pod after
   the release it waited for; a pod's unprepare after its prepare;
   optional prepares: a pod starting at a controller kill, a pod
   deleted while starting or unblocking) and the hosts of "any" pods:
   the distinct outcomes are the alternatives of the settled state,
   the issue order first. Blocked pods stay blocked while the hole is
   there; when it clears they are UNBLOCKING: Running or
   ContainerCreating accepted, Running within --unblock-timeout of the
   settle that first saw it so (on-line). Faults make no region
   events. Generation rules: off-line every stack keeps one outcome
   per window (no race for the order); nothing changes a stack where a
   pod is unblocking (its retry lands on top) but deleting that pod;
   --avoid-holes leaves out actions with an outcome that adds a hole;
   lease-get needs nothing in flight on its hb0 stack and the local
   region there or no hole (else it would fail after --wait 60s).
R16 Containerd restart (A8, bug-01 fix): the driver restarts in place
   like after driver-kill (restarts +1, F reseeded, the kill window,
   steering as R8), containers keep running.

5. The oracle
-------------
A settle observation is checked against the predicted state and each
alternative; the first that fits (else the closest: wait before fail,
fewest differences) gives the verdict and the adopted state. Per
alternative: index the pods (slot label or name, generation), resolve
(node of "any" pods, either, maybe attachments, steering "either",
unblocking pods seen Running with their region on top, unblock times,
driver pod names, lease IDs), then O1 pods (incl. blocked: Pending,
bound, claim allocated/Attached, newest Warning event containing "CXL
decoders released out of order"; unblocking: ContainerCreating until
the deadline), companions, others, O8 controllers and drivers, O2
attachments (owners k8s: only, A3), per VM O3 memdevs, regions, nodes,
O4 state files and CDI, O5 cpuset.mems and memory.high, the decoder
stacks against "ports" (A10: the host bridge port's committed
decoders in index order, region names mapped through the regions'
serials, "" = hole; unblocking pods' regions may be on top), O6 env
and lease lists. Without "ports" the stack is not checked and a race
for the order adopts the issue order.
Stable rule: "pending" (wait; after the settle timeout the last wait
is the failure) unless it can never become right, then "fatal" (fail
at once): restarts above the model's; a pod of an unknown slot or
generation, an old generation not deleting, two pods of one
generation; Failed/Succeeded while not deleting (A7); Running when it
must be pending, waiting for the attach or blocked; wrong node, also of
a companion; an adopted attachment (any owner), a failed one (k8s
owners); a wrong env or lease list (no second inspection); an
unblocking pod not Running after --unblock-timeout; wrong
write/read/lease-get results. Messages "AREA: what, want what (+N
more)".

6. Decisions where the contract was silent
------------------------------------------
- Step 1 of an on-line run is a settle; the teardown steps (R14) are
  steps (in mbt-steps.txt, checked) that ignore MBT_UNSETTLED. A
  replayed file that ends with its teardown gets no second one.
- settle is enabled only with operations in flight; write and read
  are synchronous checks: allowed when forced, not counted.
- ctrl-kill not while an "any" pod of the pool holds its device on a
  node not known yet; ctrl-restart/ctrl-kill only when up, ctrl-start
  only when down; driver-restart not in a kill window.
- Off-line: no "any" slots; controller-kill and stack races are not
  generated (R5, R15); what stays open (a maybe attachment, steering
  either, never-written data, unblocking pods) stays open in the
  expectations until an event decides it. --from stops with the step
  number where a step needs what an on-line run adopted.
- The Expectation is the model state (slots, controllers, drivers,
  data, stacks) plus its alternatives and the done prediction; the
  oracle derives the rest, so check needs no model of its own.
- Plan reuse: the plan made ahead is used if the adopted state has the
  same abstraction (String) and the action is enabled; else a new
  plan. With --plan-time 0 the plan ahead is never cut, so the same
  seed and SUT give the same steps (tested).
- GOFMBT review (05-review-GOFMBT F1/F2): no state is changed after
  gofmbt saw it (adoption builds new states); the step being executed
  is not marked covered before its observation: planning ahead
  searches from the state before it with the predicted step as the
  forced first step (planAfter, depth+1), and the step is marked with
  its adopted end state afterwards; ResetCoverage only when the search
  completed the depth and found nothing.
- Names: devices "a-pooled0" or "pooled0"; companions' requester and
  inspect keys slot name or label; CDI files by substring;
  localDevices serial number, decimal or 0x string or serialHex; a
  decoder's region by name through the VM's regions list.
- Profiles tiny (tests) and union (full + small: EXEC's stub lists).
- Coverage weights: CNxW counts each string W times (prefixed copies).
  C3 weight 1 is gofmbt's CoverActionFormatCombinations(2) on formats
  "verb kind" (kind family only): 51 formats.
- State.String() is the C4 abstraction: per pool device its users by
  kind and phase class (new, run, att, wait, gone, blk, unb), per
  local memdev fill and users, per stack the sequence of kinds (local,
  pooled, shared, hole), per driver up/killed/restarted and F, per
  controller up/down/down-maybe. Memoized with atomics.
- The binary sets GOMAXPROCS=2 and GOGC=400 unless set (P4: the GC of
  the 256-CPU host spent most of the time on its own synchronization;
  planning 3x faster).

9. C2 and C4 and "blocked" (orchestrator's question)
----------------------------------------------------
Implemented: (1) a fault's C2 strings carry the phase of every pod it
affects, so "fault driver-kill xnuma blocked", "fault ctrl-kill sdax
unblocking", "fault kubelet-restart ldax blocked" are contexts of
their own; (2) a fault on a VM with a hole on a host bridge adds
"fault VERB hole hbN", a pool fault "fault VERB hole" when a host has
a hole on the pool's host bridge, so faults while a hole exists count
also without a blocked pod; (3) C4 has "stack vmX/hbN local,pooled,
hole,shared"-like shapes and the phase classes blk/unb in the device
and local-memdev strings, so reaching a hole, a blocked pod and its
unblocking are new abstract states. Recommendation: keep blocked as a
phase (not a kind) in C2 - the interesting question is what a fault
does to a pod whose Prepare is being retried (driver restart: does
the retry still come; kubelet restart: is the claim prepared twice;
ctrl-kill: is its attachment kept). The generated full runs reach it
(600 steps off-line: 25 settles with a blocked pod, 6 unblocking).

10. Contract: ambiguities and proposed amendments
-------------------------------------------------
P1 (5) generate emits mbt-expect for settle, write, read, lease-get;
   other steps fail on a nonzero status in bash (EXEC does so).
P2 (5) check: --since UNIX_SECONDS of the first poll (or --elapsed).
