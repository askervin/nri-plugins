G1  bug  bug-11/bug-12: the hollow region of a cut create-region leaks
    when kubelet prepares the claim again
  The record names no region, and a hollow region is not listed by
  the memdev (M/memctl.go:229-246). It is deleted only by the sweep of
  releaseDevices with unfinished=true (D/pool_persistent.go:373-385),
  i.e. by unprepareUnfinished (D/pool.go:961) or a rollback. kubelet's
  normal answer to a Prepare cut by a restart is to prepare the claim
  again (71-90 s); that Prepare finds no region of the memdev, creates
  a new one, succeeds and forgets the record (D/pool.go:635). The
  claim's later Unprepare is that of a prepared claim (unfinished
  false): no sweep. The hollow region stays until a reboot or a manual
  delete_region, holding its address range in the window (8 GiB per
  host bridge in the e2e VMs; a cut after set_size, ndctl
  cxl/region.c:722, and before set_target, :745). Each such cut costs
  a device's worth of window; the scan warns once per start.
  R1 TestRevCutCreateRegionRetryLeaksHollow (TestPoolCutCreateRegion
  with "Prepare again" instead of "Unprepare"): prepared, unprepared,
  released, wantClean ok, then
    hollow region1 left after the Prepare again and the Unprepare:
    cxl create-region -t ram -d decoder0.0 -m mem0
    cxl disable-region region0 / destroy-region region0 /
    disable-memdev mem0
  bug-11 5 "Left" says the region "is deleted after the release of
  the unfinished claim"; that is only the Unprepare branch.
  Fix: name the region in the record before it exists. Before a new
  region is created (Regions(memdev) empty) the pool driver can find
  the root decoder itself (RootDecoder), read the name of the next
  region from <root decoder>/create_ram_region or create_pmem_region
  (K/region.c:2774; create-region writes that name back, and the
  kernel's cmpxchg at :2811 refuses any other with EBUSY), save it in
  the record (one more fsync'd save), and pass the decoder to
  CreateRegion/CreatePmemRegion. Then: at a Prepare again, after the
  device is prepared, delete the recorded region if it is hollow and
  is not the device's region; at an unfinished Unprepare and a
  rollback, delete only that region (also when the memdev is gone,
  which today deletes nothing for an unfinished device). This also
  replaces the sweep of G2.

G2  risk  bug-12: the sweep deletes hollow regions of other owners, on
    every failed Prepare
  releaseDevices runs the sweep for every serial it releases when
  unfinished is true, and rollback always passes true
  (D/pool.go:713), with or without a record: every failed Prepare
  (memdev timeout, a create-region error, a refused reader, S13 of
  11-findings-persistent.txt) deletes every hollow region under the
  device's root decoder (M/decoders.go:213-222), not only one that a
  cut create-region of this device left. bug-12 5e gives the reason
  not to do that in cxl.nri.io ("their owner is unknown (a region
  being set up by the local allocator, which runs concurrently, or by
  another tool)"); the same applies here. In the e2e VMs the pool
  ports and the local memdev 0xc100e2e1 are under the one host bridge
  cxlhb0, so one root decoder (cxl-fmw.0, decoder0.0).
  The guard is DeleteHollowRegion's two samples 5 poll intervals
  apart (M/decoders.go:252-262), not a check at each poll as its
  comment and bug-12 5c say. For a ram region the uuid reads empty
  (K/region.c:156-157) and the resource is 0xffffffffffffffff until
  set_size, so the identity cannot tell a region from a new one of
  the same name, and the kernel reuses the recently freed region id
  on purpose (K/region.c:2519-2530). A create-region of the local
  allocator that stalls 1 s before its target (a loaded VM), or a
  destroy and a create in that second, loses its region and fails.
  Each hollow region also costs 1 s under p.mu on every rollback, and
  one whose delete_region fails is retried, and logged, on every
  rollback.
  R2 TestRevRollbackDeletesForeignHollow: region7 hollow under
  decoder0.0 (no record names it), a Prepare whose create-region fails
  with an error (no kill):
    cxl disable-memdev mem0
    delete_region decoder0.0 region7
  Fix: G1's recorded name. Without it at least: sweep only when the
  claim has a record from a cut Prepare (loaded at start) or when the
  create-region of this Prepare was killed (ctx done); poll at every
  interval and require size and resource unchanged at each.

G3  risk  bug-12: a hollow region with online memory is deleted
  A numa device removed while its memory is online (a forced pod
  deletion; the guest lets a type-3 device go, kmem keeps the memory
  "until the next reboot") leaves a hollow region over online blocks.
  The Unprepare of its prepared claim finds the memdev gone and
  deletes the recorded region (D/pool_persistent.go:381-382):
  DeleteHollowRegion checks commit and targets only. memctl refuses to
  destroy a region whose blocks stay online (M/memctl.go:1118-1127,
  "the memory would read garbage"); this path skips that rule. The
  kmem resource probably keeps the range busy after remove_resource,
  so I do not claim corruption, but "it maps no memory" (pool.md, the
  log text, bug-12 5d) is not true here.
  R9 TestRevHollowWithOnlineMemory: prepared numa claim, mem0
  unplugged with memory74/75 online_movable, region0 hollow:
    hollow region0 deleted while its memory blocks are online_movable
  Fix: in DeleteHollowRegion refuse (with the delete_region command in
  the error) while any block of RegionBlocks(region) is not offline.

G4  risk  bug-11 (inherited from prepared claims): the revoke of the
    first Unprepare after a restart can be skipped
  The pool kubelet plugin registers at D/driver.go:452; NRI starts at
  :474 and the lender is set at :492. A kubelet Unprepare that failed
  with Unavailable while the plugin restarted (bug-11 3) is retried
  as soon as the plugin registers. In that window lender.revoke finds
  no node lender, logs a warning and returns nil
  (D/lending.go:181-186), releasing() tells no broker, and the memory
  goes offline while the node is in the borrower's cpuset.mems: bug-11
  5d "revoked before any memory goes offline" does not hold. Prepared
  companion claims have the same window; the unfinished ones meet it
  more often, since their Unprepare is typically that first retry.
  R11 TestRevUnfinishedRevokeBeforeLending: the numa companion cut
  after the lend, restarted, unprepared before lender.set:
    Unprepare nil, memory offline, node(s) [2] still lent to the
    borrower
  ("lending unavailable: NRI not connected, cannot revoke nodes [2]").
  Fix: tell "not set yet" from "NRI did not start" in
  companionLender, and fail the Unprepare (kubelet retries) in the
  first case; or register the pool plugin after lending.set.

G5  nit  bug-11: forgetPreparing forgets before it has saved
  D/pool.go:784-786 deletes the claim from p.preparing, then saves.
  If the save fails the Unprepare fails, but kubelet's retry finds no

bug-11
- Order in prepareClaim: checkClaim and lender.check (broker only),
  then per result notePreparing (D/pool.go:553) before inflight and
  prepareDevice; nothing touches a device before the record. A save
  error fails the Prepare with nothing touched
  (TestPoolPreparingSaveFails) and restores the old record in memory.
- writeJSONFile: tmp file, fsync, rename, fsync of the directory; the
  tmp names of the two files differ; both files in one directory.
- A kill between sync and forgetPreparing leaves both files: load
  keeps the claim prepared and drops the record (M9). A kill between
  the CDI write and the state save leaves a record and a CDI device
  that start() rewrites away.
- A kill inside the Unprepare of an unfinished claim (after destroy-
  region) and two restarts before it: the record is loaded again and
  the next Unprepare finishes (R5). An Unprepare again finds nothing
  and runs no command (TestPoolCutPersistentPrepare).
- Shared serial: claim a1 prepared (fs direct), b1 cut after its
  cached node mount: the start unmounts b1's mount, b1's Unprepare
  runs no command, keeps the region and a1's node mount (R4). The
  guards are preparedDevice, mountUsed and leaseBindUsed, as for every
  release; the hollow sweep runs only after a release that was not
  skipped. Two unfinished claims of one serial: the first Unprepare
  releases the region, the second Prepare again creates it (R6); no
  container of either can run (no CDI device).
- Companion: the revoke comes before the offlining with the node found
  at start (TestPoolCutCompanionPrepare checks the memory is online
  at the revoke); changeLend also revokes a lend from before the
  restart that only the cgroup shows (D/lend.go:770-773). The lease
  bind <FsDir>/<Name> is derived only with an fs device and a valid
  name; OnReleasing marks a Lending lease Failed.
- Holders: unfinishedDevices takes the block device of the region now
  (not a stale recorded name, S5) and sets the node mount only when
  checkNodeMount accepts it, so a foreign mount is never unmounted and
  then holds the device through waitNoHolder.
- Start: records of prepared claims and of claims with no memdev on
  the node are dropped and the file saved; the numa node is read from
  the region (target_node, else the blocks) and fenced before any
  Prepare (driver.go:399, M10); the serials are pool serials for the
  publisher and the local guard.
- kubelet: K8S v1.36.4 pkg/kubelet/cm/dra/manager.go:332-365
  checkpoints the claim before NodePrepareResources, so an Unprepare
  or a Prepare again does come.
- Locks: all new state is used under p.mu (Prepare, Unprepare) or at
  construction; unfinishedNodes under serialsMu; hollowRegionLog has
  its own mutex. -race -count=3 clean.

bug-12
- Kernel: an unset target reads "\n" and targetN exists for
  N < interleave_ways only (K/region.c:852-871, :2484-2494); the C/
  loop stops at the first missing target and skips empty ones (M1).
- A region without an address range (created, not sized: ndctl sets
  the size after granularity, ways and uuid, cxl/region.c:696-722)
  reads size "0x0" (the kernel's vsprintf gives "%#llx" of 0 the
  prefix, lib/vsprintf.c number(), unlike C) and resource
  0xffffffffffffffff (K/region.c:618-633, :762-776): it scans as a
  disabled region without targets, hollow, skipped (R8).
- delete_region finds the region by id and exact name and unregisters
  it synchronously (K/region.c:2873-2896, :2559-2579), so the stat
  after the write is right; a committed region or one with a target
  is refused before the wait and after it (M5); a missing region is
  not an error.
- RegionRootDecoder: the parent of the region's real sysfs path, which
  is always a root decoder in the kernel's layout.
- The log of a hollow region is once per path and address while it
  stays; it names the right delete_region file.

bug-13
- unix.LoopInfo64 matches struct loop_info64 (include/uapi/linux/
  loop.h:52-66); lo_rdevice is huge_encode_dev(stat.rdev)
  (drivers/block/loop.c:1329), which unix.Major/Minor decode; a
  regular backing file has rdev 0 and keeps its path.
- Opening a loop device that a mount holds does not clear it: an
  autoclear device is cleared only at disk_openers == 0
  (loop.c:1752-1774).
- DEVNAME from /sys/dev/block/M:m/uevent under the plugin's sysfs
  root; any error falls back to the sysfs path, logged at V(3).
- Every comparison of backing paths goes through LoopBackingFile and
  sameDevice (checkNodeMount, Holder, bindOrigin); mountinfo sources
  are not affected. A fallback "/x" for a file named like the device
  only makes Holder report a holder (the safe direction).
- d_path of a file in a mount that the reader cannot reach is printed
  from that mount's root, without "(unreachable)": "/pmem1", as the
  evidence shows.
