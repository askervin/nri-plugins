Bugs (bug-NN files)

  01 containerd restart loses the driver's NRI connection for good
     FIXED (exit on loss, restarted by the DaemonSet), verified V14, V5
  02 Linux: a region destroyed below the top of a port's decoder stack
     blocks new regions there (design limit, also on real hardware)
     NOT a driver bug; memctl names the hole; the model predicts it
  03 Linux: freed decoders keep stale sysfs (cosmetic)        upstream
  04 a steered container stays throttled (memory.high) after a driver
     crash; X2: unmanaged steered containers never fenced
     FIXED (release + fence at Synchronize, steeredContainers.json),
     verified V14
  05 NRI updates before a container has started are lost (containerd
     applies them to the spec only): a numa lease lost on requester
     restart; a fence update in the Synchronize window
     FIXED (leases seeded at CreateContainer, PostStartContainer,
     comparisons with the real cgroup), case 1 verified V5
  06 the node's ResourceSlice gone 20-30 s after a kubelet restart
     FIXED (own slice controller, SyncDelay 1 s), verified V5
  07 split, non-atomic persistence of prepared claims (review)
     FIXED (atomic writes, reconciliation at start), unit tests
  08 kube-scheduler: a pending pod waits ~5 min for a device that a
     deleted pod held during its binding wait    upstream (Kubernetes)
  09 a shared pool device attached but not prepared was published as a
     local, sliceable memdev while its pool's slices were gone (severe:
     a local claim could online shared memory as RAM)
     FIXED (sticky pool serials in poolSerials.json, hold-back, a pool
     watch every 30 s, a pool-serial check before any local region or
     onlining; two reviews, 05-review-DRV2.txt); found by L6, unit
     tests reproduce it; not reproduced on purpose in the cluster (the
     window is narrower than a step, 06-runs.txt V9)
  10 races in pkg/cxl/memctl/pmem.go behind a flaky unit test: a disk
     node read during a pfn probe; the pfn mode read too early, which
     could make a writable attach reformat a writer's namespace
     FIXED (also: create-namespace -f only on a region verified to have
     no fsdax namespace), stress-tested
