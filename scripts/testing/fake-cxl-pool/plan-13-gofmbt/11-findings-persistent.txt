1.6 What happens to mounts and binds
container crash (restartPolicy Always)
  - fs claim: the container's mount namespace and bind go; kubelet
    creates the new container from the same CDI device: a new bind of
    the node mount, which stays mounted (no Unprepare) [I].
  - block claim: the same device node again; data written and synced
    stays; a crash cannot lose buffered writes of the guest's bdev
    page cache (they belong to the VM, not the process) [I].
  - fs lease requester: its leases are kept [R Doc/leases.md:565-572];
    the new container's /run/cxl/fs is an rbind of lease-fs/<uid>
    made at creation, which copies the existing bind [I, untested].
    Holder processes die with the container (PID namespace) [I].
  - block lease requester: the fd is gone; "cxl-request fd --lease L
    --exec ..." gives it again [R Doc/leases.md:565-572].
pod delete
  - kubelet stops the containers, then NodeUnprepareResources; the pod
    stays Terminating until it succeeds [R P13/01-findings-driver.txt:
    574-578]. releaseDevices: unbindStale; unmount the node mount
    unless another prepared claim uses it (busy: error, the serial is
    not released); per serial that no other prepared claim has: wait
    up to 10 s (one deadline per call) until nothing holds /dev/pmemK,
    then offline, disable-region, destroy-region, disable-memdev;
    labels and data untouched [R D/pool_persistent.go:250-366,
    M/memctl.go:1065-1134, Doc/usage.md:785-792].
  - Holder = a loop device backed by /dev/pmemK or an O_EXCL open that
    gets EBUSY (mounts and container binds); a plain open fd is none
    [R D/mount.go:263-281, Doc/leases.md:610-617].
  - lease requester pod: CloseClaim releases its leases and waits 5 s
    ("still releasing N leases, retry") [R D/lease_claims.go:228-260];
    companions are garbage collected; the companion's Unprepare
    unbinds without EBUSY (the requester's containers are gone) [R
    P7/31-dynamic-bind-mount-findings.txt 4].
  - The controller detaches after deallocation, i.e. after the last
    Unprepare [R P13/01-findings-driver.txt 3.9].
lease-put of an fs lease
  - revoke, then unbind (never MNT_DETACH); a requester with a file of
    the fs open or mapped makes it EBUSY: Unprepare fails, kubelet
    retries about every second, the lease stays Releasing, the
    companion Terminating, the data stays readable; after the holder
    ends, lease and companion are gone 4.4-8.6 s later [R
    D/pool.go:785-806, D/pool_persistent.go:489-520,
    P7/34-dynamic-bind-mount-status-T.txt:257-271, T16:916-983].
lease-put of a block lease: no holder is seen for an fd; the region
  is released under an open fd, whose I/O fails (ENXIO, EIO) [R
  Doc/leases.md:610-617].
driver-kill in place, containerd-restart (A8: exit on NRI loss)
  - node mounts and lease binds are kernel mounts made in the driver
    container and propagated to the host by mountPropagation
    Bidirectional (deployment/helm/dra-driver-cxl/templates/
    daemonset.yaml:112); they outlive the container [R
    Doc/leases.md:629-635; I for the CRI DaemonSet: only a ctr
    container without propagation was measured, and its mounts
    vanished, P7/24-erofs-status-D.txt:405-411]. Never measured in e2e.
  - restoreMounts (1.5) keeps them; the CDI specs are deleted and
    written again at start [R Doc/usage.md:1496-1498].
  - A Prepare in flight: region, namespace, node mount or bind may
    exist without the claim in the state file; the restart unmounts
    the orphan mount and bind (1.5 c, d) and keeps the region;
    kubelet's retry reuses the region (M/pmem.go:365-370), finds the
    namespace, mounts again. If the pod was deleted first, the
    Unprepare of a claim the driver does not know returns nil [R
    D/pool.go:786-789]: the region stays (S1).
  - An Unprepare in flight, killed after the unmount: the claim is
    still in the file and the restart mounts it again (b); killed
    after the region release: (b) fails and removes the directory, the
    retried Unprepare unmounts nothing, its holder check passes
    (ENOENT/ENXIO = gone), the memdev release is a no-op [R
    D/mount.go:271-279, M/memctl.go:1065-1079]; see S5.
  - containers, their binds and block fds are not affected [I].
driver-restart (pod deleted): helper.Stop only, nothing unmounted [R
  D/pool.go:315-319]; otherwise as driver-kill.
kubelet-restart: no Prepare again; containers keep running; kubelet
  may re-create a container (contract M2): it binds the node mount
  again [I]; failed Unprepares are retried after it [I].
ctrl-kill, ctrl-start, ctrl-restart: attachments stay (only the
  controller detaches); new allocations as A18-A20; a writer's
  exclusive attach waits (Attaching) [R P13/04-contract.txt A18-A20].
server-restart: files untouched, attachments reloaded, devices stay
  in qemu [R F/pkg/server/server.go:331-339, P13/01-findings-driver.
  txt 3.4].

1.7 Across VMs: one writer, readers elsewhere
- pool-c reaches vm0, vm1, vm2 under hb1 [R T/model/topology.go:
  133-140, T/code.var.sh:171]. One pair of host files backs the device
  in every qemu (share=on): a write synced in one guest is in the host
  file at once and in every later region of any guest [R
  Doc/pool.md:156-162; I coherence of a shared file mapping].
- The writer's claim takes all shares: the scheduler allocates no
  other claim of the device anywhere; the server refuses an exclusive
  attach while the device is attached elsewhere, and any attach while
  it is attached exclusively; a shared attachment on the same host
  becomes exclusive in place [R F/pkg/server/ops.go:540-545,
  :904-920]. So the writer is Pending until every reader claim is
  deallocated, i.e. after every reader node's Unprepare unmounted its
  node mounts and released its region; a cached mount's page cache
  goes with its autoclear loop device [R D/pool_persistent.go:322-366;
  I cache drop at loop clear].
- direct readers map device memory (no page cache): always the device
  content; cached readers: one page-cache copy per node through the
  loop device (direct I/O over pmem, no second copy) [R
  P7/21-erofs-findings.txt F8, F9]; valid because the content cannot
  change while a mount exists. A ro block reader fills the page cache
  of /dev/pmemK of its VM; it goes with the region [I].
- Labels are written only by the driver's ndctl create-namespace for
  a writer of a device without fsdax namespace; every kernel reads
  them at region probe; readers change neither data nor labels (sha256
  of both files equal after the readers) [R T16:1008-1009,
  P7/21-erofs-findings.txt F5].
- Defined behaviour: a reader sees the erofs that the last writer
  synced before its claim was released; an fs reader of a device
  without namespace or erofs is refused (retried, not failed); a ro
  block reader needs only the namespace; a rewrite under mounted
  readers cannot happen. A reader that comes before any writer is
  refused and its share keeps every writer out: the device is stuck
  until that pod is deleted (by design of shares, worth a doc line)
  [I from D/pool_persistent.go:123-127 and the share capacity].

3. A model extension
--------------------
3.1 Devices and resources
- DevID is pool*2 (+1) today [R T/model/topology.go:104-130]: make it
  a table per pool (pool-c: c-pooled0, c-shared0, c-pmem0, c-pmem1);
  Device gains Persistent; CtrlState.Att [2][nVMs] becomes per device
  of the pool; State.Data (volatile tokens) stays, PDev is new per
  persistent device. nRes = nDevs + nVMs = 11 still fits the uint16
  masks Held and Contested [R T/model/state.go:250-260].
- RKey devKey(d) works unchanged; RKey.kind() gets "pmem" [R
  T/model/stack.go:46-70].
3.2 Kinds (pool-c)
  pwr-c   writer: c-pmem0 whole (all shares), block rw; creates the
          namespace on a fresh device (capacity like sexcl)
  pfsd-c  a share, fs direct, default path /run/cxl-fs/C1AE2002
  pfsc-c  a share, fs cached, path /models
  pbro-c  a share, block read-only
  pbad-c  a share with block rw: always Refused by the config; holds
          its share (blocks writers), never a region
  xpb-c   c-pmem1 (non-shared) block rw; xpf-c c-pmem1 fs direct
  plse    a lease claim whose leases are persistent: lease-get modes
          pbw (block writer, whole c-pmem0), pbr (block ro share),
          pfd (fs direct share, --name mbt), pfc (fs cached share,
          default name = lease id)
  Kind.pooled() relies on kind order (k <= KSExcl, T/model/kinds.go:
  45-46): the new kinds need explicit predicates.
3.3 Slots
  profile "pmem" (first trials, 11): pwr-c@vm0#0 pwr-c@vm2#0
    pfsd-c@vm1#0 pfsd-c@vm2#0 pfsc-c@vm1#0 pfsc-c@vm2#0 pbro-c@vm0#0
    plse@vm1#0 plse@vm2#0 sdax-c@vm0#0 xnuma-c@vm1#0 (volatile hb1
    neighbours for A9)
  profile "fullp" = full + pwr-c@vm0#0 pwr-c@vm2#0 pwr-c@any#0
    pfsd-c@vm1#0 pfsd-c@vm2#0 pfsd-c@vm2#1 pfsd-c@any#0 pfsc-c@vm0#0
    pfsc-c@vm1#0 pfsc-c@vm2#0 pbro-c@vm1#0 pbad-c@vm0#0 plse@vm0#0
    plse@vm1#0 plse@vm2#0 (+ xpb-c@vm1#0 xpf-c@vm0#0 with c-pmem1)
  Why: both accesses on two VMs (one node mount per access, shared by
  claims), a claim and a lease of one access on vm2 (pfsd-c@vm2 and
  plse@vm2 pfd), two claims of one access on vm2 (mountUsed), a
  writer on each side, any-node slots for adoption.
3.4 State additions
- PDev per persistent device: NS Tri (no, yes, maybe), FS (none,
  erofs, maybe), Tok and Raw Token, UUID string (adopted). Start:
  NS no, FS none, Raw "" (zeros).
- SlotState: Hold (fs claim or fs lease holds a file mapped), the
  lease modes above, LeaseBusy (lease-put while held: Releasing).
- Phase Refused: allocated and attached, its Prepare refused by the
  device (no namespace, no erofs) or the config (pbad); Pending,
  ContainerCreating, retried; holds its resource. Distinct from
  Blocked (A9), which wins when both apply.
3.5 Content tokens
- pwrite img by a writer (claim or pbw lease) at step N: Tok = Raw =
  "mbt-<N>", FS erofs; pwrite junk: FS none, Raw "". Status 0.
- A writer's Prepare: NS no -> yes (UUID adopted from the observer);
  NS maybe -> adopted from pdevs.lsa.
- pread: fs kinds read Tok (a Running fs reader implies FS erofs);
  block kinds read Raw. pwrite on a read-only kind fails and changes
  nothing.
- A starting fs reader becomes Running iff NS yes and FS erofs when
  its Prepare runs, else Refused; pbro iff NS yes. While any claim
  other than the writer's holds the device, PDev cannot change, so a
  reader always reads the current PDev.
- Readers, crashes and every fault leave PDev unchanged; pwipe sets
  NS no, FS none, Raw "".
- pdevs (host) == PDev at every settle; data and LSA hashes change
  only at a writer's Prepare on NS no, at pwrite and at pwipe.
3.6 Predictions per fault and phase
  (W writer claim, R fs/block reader claim, L persistent lease,
  "as volatile" = the A9-A21 rules of a shared device apply)
  create   W: Starting -> Running (exclusive attach; NS yes); Pending
             while a share is held anywhere
           R: Starting -> Running or Refused (3.5); Pending while a
             writer holds the device
  delete   W/R from Running: Unprepare; node mount gone with the last
             claim of its access on the VM, region with the last claim
             of the device on the VM; no holder can exist (containers
             gone); attachment removed after deallocation
           from Starting: as volatile; NS maybe if a fresh device's
             writer may have prepared
           from Refused: nothing to release (each retry rolled back)
  crash    W/R Running: Restarting -> Running, same env and node/bind;
             mounts and region unchanged; Hold cleared
           L requester: leases kept; fs bind visible again in the new
             container; block fd gone (pread through fd works: fd op)
  hold/unhold  L fs: lease-put while Hold -> LeaseBusy: lease
             Releasing, companion Terminating, bind and node mount
             stay, pread still works; unhold, crash or delete ->
             released within 60 s; R: Hold has no effect on Unprepare
  driver-kill, containerd-restart (in place), driver-restart (new pod)
           Running/Refused/LeaseBusy: mounts, binds, loops, regions
             unchanged; driver Ready again (A15); Refused retries go
             on; LeaseBusy stays until unhold
           Starting: the claim prepares after the restart (region
             reused); a delete in the same window: no region left
             (defined; the SUT leaks today, S1)
           Deleting: Unprepare completes after the restart; nothing
             left
  kubelet-restart: no change; a container may be re-created (Slack,
             M2): it gets its bind again
  ctrl-kill/start/restart, server-restart: as volatile shared device;
             W Starting while ctrl down: Attaching; PDev unchanged
  pwipe    only with no attachment of the device: PDev fresh
  driver-kill-at: as driver-kill at a known point of a Prepare of the
             slot that is Starting; then delete or not
3.7 Decoder stacks (A9)
- c-pmem0's region on vm/hb1 lives from the first prepared claim of
  the device on the VM (static or companion) to the Unprepare of its
  last one that succeeds: as c-shared0 (T/model/stack.go:24-27);
  LeaseBusy and holders only move that Unprepare later.
- A Refused claim's every retry creates and destroys the region
  (rollback, D/pool.go:442-461). All pool Prepares and Unprepares of
  a node run under one lock (D/pool.go:394-401, :775-783) and hb1
  carries pool-c only, so that push and pop are at the top [I]: accept
  the region present or absent, and let nothing else change that
  stack while a Refused claim exists (like waitsRetry,
  T/model/state.go:139-141), or forbid Refused next to pool-c
  region changes on the same VM in generate.
- A hole on vm/hb1 blocks persistent Prepares like any other (Blocked,
  the memctl text); a refused-to-be reader is Blocked first.
- Concurrent Prepares on hb1 are serialized by the driver but their
  order is the kubelets': adopt as A10.
3.8 Nondeterministic outcomes to adopt
- NS after a writer deleted, or the driver killed, while Starting on
  a fresh device: from pdevs.lsa ("#adopt pns=c-pmem0:yes").
- namespace UUID, pmem name, loop device: adopted at first sight,
  then constant while the region lives (UUID forever until pwipe).
- a Refused claim's transient region: either.
- end of a busy release: Releasing until the holder ends, then gone
  within 60 s (retries ~1 s, done 4.4-8.6 s).
- driver-kill-at: whether the state was saved before the kill (both
  converge while the pod stays).
- the CDI rewrite at driver start: a fs/block container (re)created
  in that window may fail once (CreateContainerError) and come back
  later: extra Slack or wait [I; Doc/usage.md:1496-1498].
- any-node slots: the node (as today).
3.9 New invariants (checked at settle)
  P1 node mounts on a VM = one per (serial, access) of the fs claims
     and fs companions prepared there; direct: erofs on the namespace's
     /dev/pmemK with dax=always; cached: erofs on a loop device with
     backing /dev/pmemK, dio 1, ro 1, without dax=always; ro,nosuid,
     nodev; none else under mounts/ (no stale node mount)
  P2 lease binds = one per Ready or LeaseBusy fs lease, at lease-fs/
     <lease claim uid>/<name>, same source as its node mount, ro,
     nosuid,nodev; the requester's /run/cxl/fs/<name> exists in its
     container; no other mount under lease-fs (no bind left)
  P3 loop devices over pmem = the cached node mounts of the VM
  P4 a pmem region of a persistent device on a VM iff a claim of it
     is prepared there (Refused: either); its namespace fsdax map mem
     when NS yes; that block device = poolClaims blockDevice = CDI
     node path = env PATH of block claims
  P5 no namespace lost: once NS yes, every region of the device on any
     VM has the fsdax namespace with the adopted UUID; the LSA hash
     changes only at a writer's Prepare on NS no and at pwipe
  P6 data intact: pdevs token, raw and erofs == PDev after every
     step; readers and faults never change the data or LSA hash
  P7 no busy region released: dmesg has no new BUG/Oops/WARNING or
     pmem/erofs errors; bootID unchanged; no attachment of a
     persistent device in failed or leaked state
  P8 directories: lease-fs/<uid> and leases/<uid> exist iff the lease
     claim is prepared or still has a bind; no orphan dirs in mounts/
  P9 attachments: exclusive on the writer's VM only while a writer
     holds the device; shared on each VM with a reader claim
  P10 CDI and env as 1.4; fs claims have no /dev/pmem* in the
     container, block claims exactly PATH; companions no CDI device
  P11 writes: on pbro, pfs* and pbr fail; pread on readers == PDev
3.10 Coverage
- C2 strings fault|kind|phase with refused and busy phases and the
  lease modes (driver-kill|plse-pfd|busy, ctrl-kill|pwr|starting).
- C4 per persistent device: NS/FS state, holders by class (writer,
  fsd, fsc, bro, lease-fs, lease-blk), per VM the set of node mounts
  {direct, cached}.
