Summary
-------
Stage 1 (canonical key, intern table, per-plan transition cache) and
stage 2 (precomputed coverage strings) are committed; stage 3 (short
coverage keys) was measured at 0-2% and is not kept. Every seeded
experiment takes exactly the steps of the unchanged model (same
coverage numbers, verb counts, paths per depth) once the experiment's
oracle runs on a clock that does not depend on the plan time (see
"The wall clock trap" below: the protocol as written in 03-plan is not
reproducible, the unchanged model itself takes other steps when its
plans are slower). Depth 3, default mix: plan time -31% (plantime
mean 561 -> 389 ms, max 2.40 -> 1.63 s), -40%/-45% in the growth runs
(650/702 -> 387/388 ms mean). Depth 4: -12% only (5.9 -> 5.2 s): the
cache is bounded to 150k states, a depth-4 tree has ~430k. The price
is memory: a plan keeps its distinct states alive (~2 KB each, up to
~300 MB), RSS ~0.7 GB instead of 40 MB; the binary sets a soft memory
limit of 768 MiB unless GOMEMLIMIT is set. Weighted strings (C1x3,
C2x2) cost ~17% per path on top of stage 2; a struct-keyed
outcomesCut would save another 11-16% (not done: stream DEBUG's
file; the patch is ready, see "outcomesCut").

2. The key
----------
One string, fixed order, fixed-size fields except the length-prefixed
stack entries and events (injective; ~180 bytes on the full profile:
43 slots x 3 + 6 + 9 + stacks + 9 + events).
  field                     bytes    encoding
  Slots[i], every slot      3        Phase | Prev<<4
                                     Node+1 | RaceKill<<2 | Steer<<3 |
                                       Lease<<5 | LeaseMode<<7
                                     min(Restarts,15) | min(Slack,15)<<4
  Ctrl[p], every pool       2        Up | Slices<<1 | Att[d][v]<<(3+2(3d+v))
  Drv[v], every VM          3        Restarts; F | FMaybe<<4;
                                     Killed | Restarted<<1
  Stacks[si], 6 stacks      1+N/2    N, then E[j]+1 as nibbles
  Contested, Held, Released 2 each   little endian
  Unsettled                 1
  Ev, in issue order        2+7/ev   count; per event Stack+1 | Kind<<3 |
                                     Opt<<4 | Sync<<5 | Handover<<6;
                                     Key+1; User; Seq (2); After+1 (2)
  trailer                   2/slot   the exact Restarts, Slack of every
                                     slot whose nibble is 15, slot order
Ev: kept in order with Seq and After (conservative: Seq equals the
index today, After refers to Seq; normalizing would buy nothing).
Slack is in the key although only the oracle reads it (restartSlack
writes it, next() never decides on it): conservative as asked; leaving
it out would merge a few more states.
Left out, with the reason (grep of every reader, M at fa00607e1):
  Steps        only write()'s token text (into Data/SlotData), the
               protocol and messages read it
  Last         was read by the C2 function only: now the step's action
  HeldAt       oracle.go (the clock of Held; the Held bit is in the key)
  UnblockAt    oracle.go, expect.go (clocks of Unblocking/Stalled/
               Attaching)
  Drv.Pod, Drv.PodBefore, Drv.PodGen, Drv.Inc
               oracle.go, sim.go, simref.go, expect.go, dump
  LeaseIDs     oracle.go, sim.go, adopt.go, expect.go
  SlotData, Data
               tokens: expect.go, sim.go, adopt.go, oracle.go adoption
  Slots[i].Gen pod names/UIDs: oracle.go, sim.go, simref.go, expect.go
  Alts, Truncated
               the other outcomes of a settle: oracle.go, sim.go,
               simref.go, adopt.go, online.go, expect.go
  cfg          one per planner
A canonical state carries the left-out fields (and Last, Steps, Alts)
of whichever path reached its key first. Harmless: no state of the
search leaves the planner (plan/planAfter return an Act; the on-line
loop and the experiment compute the executed state with from.apply(a);
planAfter's roots from/to are the real objects and are never
modified).
Bounds checked by TestKeyLayoutBounds (nPhases <= 16, nVMs <= 3,
nDevs+2 <= 16, nBridges <= 4, nStacks+1 <= 8, users < 256).

3. The per-plan cache and its memory
------------------------------------
Scope: one planWalk per plan() (shared by its two attempts) and per
planAfter(); dropped when the call returns (nothing hangs off State
objects, so the Coverer's coveredPath retains nothing of it, F5).
Single goroutine: online.go never runs plan, planAfter and mark
concurrently; mark runs outside plans and computes its strings
directly.
Tables: states map[key]*State; steps map[*State][]*Step (the slice
handed out is a copy); planStrings.step map[*Step][]string and
.chances map[*State][]string (stage 2).
Distinct states per plan (cached states, = intern-table size):
  depth 1     32 mean       63 max
  depth 2   1319 mean     2798 max
  depth 3  32568 mean   127615 max   (plantime; paths 101882 mean:
                                      1 distinct state per 3.1 paths)
           26197 / 26958 mean, 135180 / 134404 max (growth seeds 1/2)
  depth 4 148452 mean   150000 max   (the bound; ~430k unbounded)
Live heap at a 114858-state plan (runtime.GC + HeapAlloc, heap
profile): stage 1 193 MB (1.7 KB/state: State 640 B, its Slots 576 B,
key ~200 B, map entries, its Step), stage 2 236 MB (2.05 KB/state).
Alts are negligible (392 alternative states in that plan). The bound
maxPlanStates = 150000 caps it at ~300 MB; past it new states and
their steps are walked uncached (the same steps, the old speed).
GOGC=400 (tuneRuntime, for the short-lived states of P4) lets the heap
grow to 5x the live size: unbounded, depth 4 had 3.9 GB RSS (GOGC=100:
1.35 GB, +11% time). Hence the soft limit 768 MiB (GOMEMLIMIT env
overrides it): RSS ~0.72-0.75 GB at depth 3 and 4. Measured at depth 4
(plantime, 8 plans, noisy): 768 MiB 5.0-5.9 s, 1 GiB 4.6 s (RSS 0.97
GB), no limit 4.5 s (RSS 2.1 GB); depth 3 within noise of all of them.

5. The wall clock trap (for the orchestrator)
---------------------------------------------
The experiment's oracle ran on time.Now(): checkState -> checkStateAt
(now) clears a Held device bit 6 s after the settle that set it
(oracle.go "heldSeconds"), also the unblock (330 s) and binding (420 s)
timers. So a seeded run without deadline hits is NOT reproducible
across planner speeds: the unchanged base binary with GOGC=25 (plans
928 ms instead of 667 ms) ends seed 1 at C1 186/215 (last new C1 299)
instead of 188/215; the cached planner diverged at step 277 (the plan
of step 276 made 4982 instead of 5006 StepsFrom calls), and two runs
of the same cached binary with the same flags disagreed with each
other (one took the base's steps): the oracle's timers are the only
wall-clock input of a run (the plan deadline, 60 s, is never hit).
The trees themselves were checked identical at that plan (lock-step
compare of the planner's populated cache against an uncached walk: no
difference; the StepsFrom call logs of a cached and an uncached run
byte-identical when their states are). Fix (commit d6423ce20):
--step-time D gives the oracle refEpoch + n*D; with --step-time 1s
the base trajectories of seeds 1 and 2 keep the F3 numbers up to @200
(seed 2 identically to 300) and are reproducible for any plan time.
D6 acceptance runs must use --step-time (or --max-paths) to be
meaningful; the orchestrator's F3 base numbers past step 200 of seed 1
belong to one timing.

7. Limitations
--------------
- The key must follow State: a field added to State, SlotState,
  DriverState, CtrlState or stackEvent that next() or a coverage
  function reads must go into appendKey, or into the left-out list with
  its reason AND into canon_test.go searchView/noisyTwin.
  TestCanonicalKeyComplete fails (states of equal keys differ in
  searchView) if a field is neither, provided the random trees reach
  states that differ in it.
- Memory: ~0.7 GB RSS instead of 40 MB at depth 3; depth 4 gains only
  ~12% because of the bound (430k states would need ~0.9 GB live).
- d1 plans are ~50 us slower (key + maps); irrelevant.
- The deadline: a search stopped by --plan-time depends on timing
  anyway; the cache only changes how far it gets.
- The cache is per plan: planAfter does not reuse plan's tree (F4: ~3%).
- recorded/ (the L4/L5/L7 replays of review_test.go) is untracked in
  the main checkout; the worktree has a copy (not committed) to run
  the suite.
