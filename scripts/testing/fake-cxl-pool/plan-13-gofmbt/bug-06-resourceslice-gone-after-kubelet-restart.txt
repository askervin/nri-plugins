BUG-06: THE NODE'S RESOURCESLICE IS GONE FOR 30 S AFTER KUBELET RESTART

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-24. Found by the executor (plan 13, stream
EXEC: S3 in 04-status-EXEC.txt section 12), fixed in the working tree
(uncommitted), unit tested; cluster verification pending.

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, L/ = vendor/k8s.io/dynamic-resource-
allocation/ (v0.37.0), K/ = ~/go/pkg/mod/k8s.io/kubernetes@v1.37.0/,
R/ = /tmp/mbt-exec-dev/. Line numbers of HEAD unless "new". Times in
mbt-exec.log are the host's (EEST), in the driver logs UTC; the
clocks agree within a second.

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

After "systemctl restart kubelet" on a node whose driver keeps
running, the node has no cxl.nri.io ResourceSlice for up to 30 s
(measured 20.2, 30.3 and 25.8 s). kubelet deletes every ResourceSlice
of its node when it starts, by design. The driver's ResourceSlice
controller notices the deletion at once (an informer event) but syncs
the pool only SyncDelay later: 30 s, the library default, which
kubeletplugin.Helper.PublishResources uses and offers no option for.
In that window no claim that needs a cxl.nri.io device of the node
(local memdev and numa slices, the lease device, DRAM, regions) can
be allocated: such pods stay Pending, unschedulable. The driver logs
nothing meanwhile.

Fix: the driver runs the ResourceSlice controller itself, configured
as the helper's, with SyncDelay 1 s. The slice is back about 1 s
after kubelet deletes it.

========================================================================
2. REPRODUCTION

n4-cxl-shared-2 (test90), any VM whose driver runs:

  settle
  kubelet-restart vm1      systemctl restart kubelet; the driver
                           container keeps running
  create lnuma@vm1#0       a pod with a local numa claim on vm1,
                           within 30 s of the restart
  settle

Before the fix lnuma@vm1#0 stays Pending, not schedulable (vm1
publishes no cxl.nri.io device), until the slice is back 20-30 s
after the restart; then it is scheduled and runs. By hand:

  vm1$ sudo systemctl restart kubelet
  $ kubectl get resourceslices -w \
      --field-selector spec.driver=cxl.nri.io,spec.nodeName=<node>

shows the slice deleted at once and created again up to 30 s later.

Why 20.2 and 25.8 s on vm2 but 30.3 s on vm1: the slice comes back at
the first sync of the pool due after the deletion. The deletion
schedules one 30 s later, but a sync scheduled earlier wins (the work
queue keeps the earliest time), and the controller schedules a
re-sync one minute (the mutation cache TTL) after each create:
- vm2's slice was created at 11:47:43.91 ("create ResourceSlice" in
  .vm.log): re-sync at 11:48:43.9, which re-created it (ready at
  14:48:45.30) and scheduled the next re-sync at about 11:49:44
  (step 8: ready at 14:49:44.71).
- vm1's create at 11:47:43.88 had its re-sync at 11:48:43.9, before
  the restart at 11:48:45.9: the full 30 s.

4. ROOT CAUSE

kubelet, K/pkg/kubelet/cm/dra/plugin/dra_plugin_manager.go:
- NewDRAPluginManager (:151) runs wipeResourceSlices(ctx, "" /* all
  drivers */) in the background at startup (:166-181): "When kubelet
  starts up, no DRA driver has registered yet. None of the drivers
  are usable until they come back, which might not happen at all.
  Therefore it is better to not advertise any local resources".
- wipeResourceSlices (:217) calls DeleteCollection with the field
  selector spec.nodeName=<node> (:246): the node-local slices of all
  drivers, also of a driver that keeps running and re-registers a
  second later. By design: upstream's node e2e test "must be removed
  on kubelet startup" (K/test/e2e_node/dra_test.go:758) checks it.
  Otherwise kubelet wipes a driver's slices only when its plugin
  stays unregistered for 30 s (sync, :504; defaultWipingDelay,
  K/pkg/kubelet/cm/dra/manager.go:75).

The driver: D/driver.go PublishResourceSlice (:657) called
d.helper.PublishResources (:661), the helper of kubeletplugin.Start
(:408).
- L/kubeletplugin/draplugin.go PublishResources (:1183) starts a
  resourceslice controller on the first call (StartController,
  :1211) with DriverName, KubeClient, Owner (the node), Resources,
  ErrorHandler and ReconcilePoolWithName, no SyncDelay, and calls its
  Update later. No helper Option sets SyncDelay (the options,
  :329-808; neither in upstream master of 2026-09-23).
- L/resourceslice/resourceslicecontroller.go: DefaultSyncDelay = 30 s
  (:69); Options.SyncDelay (:282, "The default is 30 seconds"). The
  informer's add, update and delete handlers schedule the pool with
  queue.AddAfter(pool, syncDelay) (:632, :650, :667). The informer
  has no periodic resync (:616). So the deletion is seen by an event
  at once, and acted on 30 s later.
- The delaying queue keeps the earliest time of an item
  (vendor/k8s.io/client-go/util/workqueue/delaying_queue.go:355-361);
  syncPool schedules a re-sync DefaultMutationCacheTTL (1 min, :62)
  after a create (:980-992): the 20-26 s cases.
- Update (:423), i.e. every publish of the driver, queues the pool at
  once (queue.Add, :430, :457): not delayed.

Other drivers: Intel's intel-resource-drivers-for-kubernetes
(~/github.com/intel/, the gpu, qat, cxl and gaudi kubelet plugins)
use helper.PublishResources as well, so they have the same 30 s.
Kubernetes' integration test runs its controller with SyncDelay
DefaultSyncDelay/factor (K/test/integration/dra/core.go:516, :538);
the library's unit test of exactly this case ("kubelet deletes the
slice", TestControllerUpdateDeleteRecreate,
dynamic-resource-allocation@v0.37.0 resourceslicecontroller_test.go
:1660-1720) uses SyncDelay 0.

The pool controllers are not affected (confirmed):
scripts/testing/fake-cxl-pool/pkg/controller/publisher.go starts its
controller (:180) with Owner nil and ReconcilePoolWithName; its slice
has a NodeSelector (:161) and no spec.nodeName, so kubelet's field
selector does not match it. EXEC saw the pool slices stay (S3).

========================================================================
5. THE FIX

New D/node_slices.go: nodeSlices publishes the node's slice with a
resourceslice controller of its own, started as the helper starts
its: driver cxl.nri.io, owner Node <node> (the controller looks up
the UID), so the same names (00000-cxl.nri.io-<node>-...) and an
existing slice is adopted, not replaced; the "ResourceSlice
controller" logger; errors to driver.HandleError marked recoverable
(recoverableSliceError: errors.Is kubeletplugin.ErrRecoverable, like
the helper's unexported recoverableError, message unchanged). And:

  SyncDelay: nodeSliceSyncDelay = 1 s     (node_slices.go:48, new)

with a comment that explains why. D/driver.go: the driver gets
slices = newNodeSlices(client, node, driver.HandleError) next to the
helper; PublishResourceSlice calls slices.publish instead of
helper.PublishResources; Shutdown calls slices.stop before
helper.Stop. The helper still serves kubelet (registration, Prepare,
Unprepare); it just publishes nothing. RBAC is unchanged (the same
calls).

Why 1 s, and its cost. SyncDelay delays only the syncs that informer
events of the driver's own slice cause; who changes that slice:
- the controller itself: each of its creates and updates comes back
  as an event, one more sync 1 s later that compares and makes no
  API call (it did the same 30 s later before);
- kubelet's wipe: one DeleteCollection, one sync, one create;
- a human.
The delay batches a burst into at most one sync per second; the node
has one pool of one slice, so at most one create or update per
second, and only while someone else keeps changing the slice. The
other purpose of the delay, an informer cache that has seen a
deletion before the sync (else a redundant delete), needs
milliseconds. A field that the API server drops does not make it
loop: the controller takes the stored slice as desired (sliceStored,
L/resourceslice/resourceslicecontroller.go:1034-1067); the unit test
checks it. The driver's own publishes (startup, udev rescans, pool
list retries: rescanAndPublish) go through Update and were and are
synced at once.

kubelet's intent is kept: a driver that does not run re-creates
nothing, and one that stays unregistered is wiped after 30 s as
before. A pod scheduled in the moment between the re-creation and the
plugin's registration with the new kubelet is only delayed: its pod
sync fails with "DRA driver cxl.nri.io is not registered"
(K/pkg/kubelet/cm/dra/manager.go:336, plugin/dra_plugin_manager.go
:280) and kubelet retries it.

Not chosen: watching the slice and calling helper.PublishResources
again on its deletion (Update syncs at once, but the helper's own
informer may not have seen the deletion yet and then nothing is
created; more code); a model prediction of the 30 s window (keeps a
real outage).

8. SEEN ON THE WAY (not this bug, not fixed)

Every publish on every node logs an error (run 8, all three driver
logs: the create at 11:47:43.9 and each later update, e.g. vm1
11:48:01.1, 11:48:20.35, 11:49:46.5):

  "DRAPlugin encountered an error." err="pool \"<node>\", slice #0:
  some fields were dropped by the apiserver, probably because these
  features are disabled: unknown"

"unknown": the library's DroppedFieldsError.DisabledFeatures knows no
feature for the dropped field. Most likely NodeAllocatableResources
(set on DRAM, region and memdev devices, D/node_state.go:671, :704,
:736; +featureGate=DRANodeAllocatableResources in
vendor/k8s.io/api/resource/v1/types.go:541), with that gate off in
the cluster. The controller then takes the stored slice as desired,
so it does not loop; but each driver publish makes one update call
that the API server stores unchanged (no event: that is also why
vm1's update at 11:48:20.35 did not shorten its 30 s). For triage:
publish the field only when the cluster stores it, or log it once.
