BUG-07: THE PREPARED CLAIMS ARE PERSISTED IN TWO FILES, NOT ATOMICALLY

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-24. Predicted by 01-findings-driver.txt
section 4 E4 (a)-(c), made more likely by the bug-01 fix (the driver
exits on every loss of NRI) and raised again by 05-review-DRV.txt D3.
Fixed in the working tree (uncommitted), unit tested; no cluster run
(no reproduction: the windows are microseconds wide).

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/. Line numbers of HEAD (ed0370b24)
unless "new".

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

The cxl.nri.io driver keeps a prepared claim in two files of its data
directory (/var/lib/kubelet/plugins/cxl.nri.io/):
  preparedClaims.json      claim UID -> the PrepareResult (CDI ids)
  preparedClaimsInfo.json  claim UID -> its devices, settings, lend
persist (D/node_state.go:1008-1025) writes the CDI spec, then
preparedClaims.json, then preparedClaimsInfo.json. A driver that
stops between the two writes leaves them disagreeing, and nothing
reconciled them at the next start. preparedClaims.json was also
written in place (os.WriteFile: truncate, then write,
D/prepared_claims.go:79, :49), unlike every other state file.

Since bug-01 the driver exits (status 1) whenever the runtime closes
its NRI connection, at any moment, from the stub's goroutine, also
while a Prepare or an Unprepare persists: what was a SIGKILL window
is now a routine event of every containerd restart during pod churn.

========================================================================
2. THE CRASH WINDOWS AND THEIR EFFECTS

(a) Between the two writes of a Prepare: preparedClaims.json has the
    claim, preparedClaimsInfo.json does not.
    - the local allocator, reconciled with the claims of the info file
      (D/driver.go:566-573), releases the claim's slices at start;
    - kubelet retries the Prepare (the crashed call never succeeded);
      prepare() finds the claim in Prepared, "Claim ... was already
      prepared, nothing to do" (D/node_state.go:904-910), and returns
      the stored CDI ids;
    - the CDI spec is built from the info (buildClaimsCDISpec skips a
      claim without info, D/node_state.go:1451-1455): the container
      gets CDI ids that no spec has and never starts (unresolvable CDI
      device), for as long as the claim is allocated.
(b) Between the two writes of an Unprepare (or of the rollback of a
    failed Prepare): preparedClaimsInfo.json has the claim,
    preparedClaims.json does not. The devices were released before
    the persist (revoke, local Release).
    - the retried Unprepare finds no prepared claim and returns nil
      (preparedInfo looks at Prepared, D/node_state.go:846-851);
    - the info entry stays for ever, also across restarts: its numa
      nodes are fenced at every start (preparedNodes, D/fence.go:154),
      its lend record and devices are taken as prepared by the NRI
      plugin (containerResults) and the broker; after a rollback that
      could not release, the local slices stay too (the local
      allocator takes the claim as prepared).
(c) During the write of preparedClaims.json: a truncated or empty
    file. Every start fails ("failed to parse prepared claims file",
    D/prepared_claims.go:64-66): the driver crash-loops until the file
    is repaired by hand. The same for the creation of an empty file
    at the first start (os.WriteFile of "{}").

========================================================================
3. THE FIX

(c) D/prepared_claims.go writePreparedClaimsToFile and the creation
    in getOrCreatePreparedClaims, and the creation in
    getOrCreatePreparedClaimsInfo (D/node_state.go), go through
    writeJSONFile (D/local.go: tmp file, then rename), as the info
    file, the local state, the lease table and the pool state do: a
    crash leaves the old file or the new one.

(a), (b): a claim is prepared only when both files have it.
D/prepared_claims.go (new) loadPreparedClaims reads both files and
reconcilePreparedClaims drops what only one has:
- only in preparedClaims.json, or with a null info: the claim was
  being prepared. It is forgotten. Its local memory is released by the
  local allocator's Reconcile (newLocalHelper, D/driver.go, now takes
  the claims of both files, through the same function), and the
  Prepare that kubelet retries prepares it from scratch. This is the
  state that a crash before the first write leaves, a window that the
  driver had already (the device work of a Prepare is done before its
  persist).
- only in preparedClaimsInfo.json: the claim was being unprepared, its
  devices are released already. It is forgotten: the state that the
  second write would have left. The retried Unprepare (or Prepare,
  after a rollback) finds nothing and does its normal work.
newNodeState (D/node_state.go) loads through it, logs a warning per
forgotten claim ("claim U: prepared without its info (the plugin
stopped in its Prepare): not prepared, its retried Prepare prepares
it again", "claim U: info of a claim that is not prepared (the plugin
stopped in its Unprepare): forgotten, its devices are released"), and
its persist at start writes both files consistently.

Not chosen: one file for both. It closes the window too, but changes
the on-disk format (preparedClaims.json is documented, and an upgrade
would need a migration of both files), for no gain over the
reconciliation, which maps either crash onto a state the driver
already handles.
Not done: fsync of the state files (a power loss rather than a
process exit), the same for all of them.

Docs: docs/memory/cxl/local.md (Restart): the slices of claims that
are not in both files are released.
