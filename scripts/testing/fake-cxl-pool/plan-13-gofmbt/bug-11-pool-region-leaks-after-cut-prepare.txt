BUG-11: A POOL REGION LEAKS AFTER A PREPARE THAT THE PLUGIN DID NOT
FINISH

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-28. Found by the executor of the model-based
test (04-status-EXEC.txt 14.7 B1, runs 3 and 6), predicted as S1 in
11-findings-persistent.txt section 4 and, for volatile pool devices, as
E2 in 01-findings-driver.txt section 4. Fixed in the working tree
(uncommitted), unit tested; not verified on the cluster.

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, M/ = pkg/cxl/memctl/,
K/ = k8s.io/kubernetes v1.36.4 pkg/kubelet/, R/ = /tmp/p13-pexec/.
Line numbers of HEAD (1178bf032) unless "new".

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

The pool driver (cxl-pool.nri.io) did all the device work of a Prepare
(memdev enable, region, fsdax namespace, node mount, lease bind, fence
and online memory, lend) before it saved the claim in
preparedPoolClaims.json. A plugin killed in between restarted without
any trace of the claim. When the claim was released before kubelet
prepared it again (pod deleted, lease put), kubelet unprepared it, the
plugin answered nil for a claim it did not know, the claim was
deallocated and the pool controller detached the device with the region
still on it. The kernel then kept the region hollow (bug-12): no target,
not committed, its address range of the window held, and cxl
destroy-region refuses it. The same window exists for volatile devices:
in mode numa the memory was left online on a node that nothing fenced
after the restart; in mode dax the region and dax device stayed.

Severity: high. Every SIGKILL, OOM kill, crash or exit on the loss of
NRI (since bug-01 the plugin exits on every containerd restart) inside
a pool Prepare (84-207 ms for a persistent device, 2.4 of
11-findings-persistent.txt) leaks a region when the claim goes away
before kubelet's retry (71-90 s). Then bug-12 stops the node's
ResourceSlice updates and any start of the plugin; the address range
stays allocated until a reboot or a manual delete_region; in the test,
pool-vm-reset restarts the VM (it counts regions).

4. ROOT CAUSE

- D/pool.go:497-558 (prepareClaim): prepareDevice (D/pool.go:693-755:
  waitMemdev, CreateRegion :719/:733, Fence and Online :738-739, or
  preparePersistent, D/pool_persistent.go:107-148: CreatePmemRegion
  :118, CreateFsdaxNamespace :132, ensureNodeMount :143),
  bindLeaseMounts :537 and lender.lend :547 run before
  p.prepared[uid] = pc and p.sync() (:559-560). Nothing on disk names
  the claim before that save.
- D/pool.go:252-276 newPoolDriver: load restores the saved claims;
  restoreMounts (D/pool_persistent.go:703-771) unmounts the node mounts
  and lease binds of no prepared claim; nothing looks at regions.

  an error, nothing is released.
- kubelet adds the claim to its checkpointed claim info cache before it
  calls NodePrepareResources (K/cm/dra/manager.go:345, :400) and calls
  NodeUnprepareResources for every cached claim of a pod that goes
  (:630, :679), also when its Prepare failed: the Unprepare does come,
  and was answered wrongly.
- After the successful Unprepare the claim is deallocated and the
  controller detaches the device; the kernel detaches the endpoint

- Volatile: the node of numa memory onlined by the cut Prepare is not

5. THE FIX

Design: a record of the claim written before a device is touched, and
released by the claim's own Unprepare. Not chosen: a sweep of the pool
regions at start. It would have to know which regions a claim that
kubelet will prepare again still needs (allocated to the node, not
prepared yet: the API and the claim status), which serials are pool
devices and never local memdevs (poolSerials.json can be lost), and it
would destroy regions that the retry creates again. The record names
exactly the serials that this plugin touched for one claim, and kubelet
guarantees either an Unprepare or a Prepare again of that claim
(section 4).

a) preparingPoolClaims.json next to the state file (D/pool.go
   poolPreparingFileName :63 new, preparingPoolClaim :249 new; a
   separate file so that preparedPoolClaims.json keeps its meaning, the
   prepared claims, for the oracle and the kill-at watcher).
   notePreparing (:733 new), called by prepareClaim right before a
   device is touched (:553 new), records the claim name, the device
   (result index, serial, shared, size, mode, read-only, persistent,
   access, path) and the lend of a companion claim, and saves the file
   atomically (writeJSONFile: tmp, fsync, rename, fsync of the
   directory). A save error fails the Prepare before the device is
   touched. A device of the same result recorded by an earlier Prepare
   of the claim is replaced, the others stay.
b) The record is forgotten (forgetPreparing :779 new) when the claim is
   saved as prepared (:635 new; a crash between the two saves leaves
   both, and prepared wins at start), or when the rollback of a failed
   Prepare released everything (:725 new). A rollback that could not (a
   holder, a busy mount) keeps it, so that the claim's Unprepare or its
   next Prepare releases the rest (this also covers S13 of
   11-findings-persistent.txt).
c) Start (loadPreparing :807 new, from newPoolDriver :312 new): the
   record of a prepared claim is dropped; so is one none of whose
   memdevs is on the node (nothing can be left on them). The others are
   logged ("claim N (uid): its Prepare did not finish (the plugin
   stopped in it): devices 0x... may have a region, a namespace or
   online memory; its Unprepare releases them, its Prepare again reuses
   them"); the NUMA node of a device in mode numa is read from sysfs
   (regionNode :857 new) and fenced at start (numaNodes :390,
   updateSerials :411); their serials are serials of the pool driver
   (Serials, for the cxl.nri.io publisher). Their node mounts and lease
   binds are unmounted by restoreMounts as before (no prepared claim
   wants them). A file that cannot be parsed is logged and taken as
   empty (the old behaviour), it does not stop the start.
d) Unprepare of a claim that is not prepared (unprepareClaim :1113 new
   -> unprepareUnfinished :936 new), with a record: for a companion
   claim the broker is told that it is releasing, the nodes that the
   cut Prepare may have lent are revoked before any memory goes offline
   (revokeUnfinished :875 new: the nodes found at start) and its lease
   bind is unmounted (unfinishedLend :891 new: <fsDir>/<name>). Then
   releaseDevices (D/pool_persistent.go:326 new) with unfinishedDevices
   (D/pool.go:908 new): the block device of a persistent device is that
   of its region now (currentBlockDevice, D/pool_persistent.go:394
   new), for the holder wait; the node mount at its path is unmounted
   only when it is the erofs of that block device for its access
   (another mount there is never touched). The memdev is released as
   for a prepared claim (offline, disable-region, destroy-region,
   disable-memdev), then the hollow regions are deleted (bug-12). An
   error fails the Unprepare (kubelet retries); success forgets the
   record. Without a record: nil, as before.
e) A Prepare again after the restart (pod not deleted): the region is
   reused as before (M/memctl.go:946-949, M/pmem.go:365-370); the
   record is replaced and forgotten when the claim is prepared. A
   Prepare again that fails releases with its rollback also what the
   earlier Prepare touched for its other results.
f) Never touched: local memdevs (a record has only serials of the
   claim's pool status data); a device that another prepared claim uses
   (preparedDevice, as for every release); a block device that is held
   (waitNoHolder; the requester of an fs lease may hold the bind, a
   stale bind then, D/pool_persistent.go:683-696). An unfinished claim
   has no CDI device: no container of it runs.

Left: a Prepare killed inside cxl create-region, before the tool set
the target, leaves a hollow region that the memdev does not list: it is
deleted after the release of the unfinished claim (bug-12). A claim
whose device was detached before its Unprepare (a forced deletion): its
hollow region has no recorded name when its Prepare did not finish; the
node scan reports it (bug-12).

Docs: docs/memory/cxl/pool.md (Ordering and failure semantics),
docs/memory/cxl/usage.md (Files on the node).

9. FOLLOW-UP AFTER REVIEW (05-review-DRV3.txt G1-G8)

Replaces 5 "Left" and corrects 5d. A Prepare killed inside cxl
create-region (after the region got its size, before its target) left
a hollow region that the memdev does not list; it was deleted only by
a sweep of the root decoder at the claim's Unprepare or a rollback, not
when kubelet prepared the claim again (the normal case), and the sweep
took any hollow region under the decoder (in the e2e VMs that also
covers the local memdev).
- The name of a new region is recorded before the tool runs: memctl
  reads it from the root decoder's create_ram_region or
  create_pmem_region under a lock of the process that every region
  creation takes (pool driver and local allocator), and the pool
  driver saves it in the claim's record (Regions), and replaces it by
  the name the tool created if they differ. The recorded regions are
  deleted when hollow: after a Prepare again of the claim (its own
  region kept), at the Unprepare of the unfinished claim (also when the
  memdev is gone), at a rollback (which keeps the record if a deletion
  failed). No other hollow region is ever deleted; the sweep is gone.
- DeleteHollowRegion takes the creation lock instead of waiting 1 s,
  and refuses while memory blocks of the region's range are online.
- 5d: an Unprepare that would revoke lent nodes before the lender is
  set up (the first retry after a restart can come before NRI) now
  fails and kubelet retries; with leases disabled the lenders are set
  up empty at start. The record is forgotten only after the file
  without it was saved; the Unprepare of a prepared claim forgets a
  record that its Prepare could not.
- Tests: TestPoolCutCreateRegion (unprepare, prepare again, a foreign
  hollow region kept), TestPoolRollbackKeepsForeignHollowRegion,
  TestPoolRegionNoteReplaced, TestPoolForgetSaveFails, the numa case of
  TestPoolCutCompanionPrepare, M/TestCreateRegionNoted.
To verify in addition: a driver-kill-at region while cxl create-region
runs (rare: the watcher fires 2 ms after the region appears) followed by
kubelet's retry: "deleted hollow regionN" after "prepared", no region
left after the Unprepare.
