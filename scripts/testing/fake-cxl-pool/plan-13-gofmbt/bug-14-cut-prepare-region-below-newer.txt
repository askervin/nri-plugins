BUG-14: THE REGION OF A CUT PREPARE IS KEPT FOR KUBELET'S RETRY AND
ITS LATE RELEASE LEAVES A DECODER HOLE

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-28. Found by run FP3 of the model-based test
(06-runs.txt; step 798 settle failed after 3 min: "stack vm0/hb1:
committed decoders [hole,c-pmem0], want [c-pmem0]"). A consequence of
the bug-11 fix (Prepare again reuses what a cut Prepare made) and the
Linux decoder-stack rule of bug-02. Fixed in the working tree
(uncommitted), unit tested, verified on the cluster by a replay that
failed before the fix.

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, M/ = pkg/cxl/memctl/,
R/ = /tmp/p13-runs/fp3/out/, B/ = /tmp/p13-runs/b14/. Line numbers of
HEAD (c74eff381) unless "new". Times of the driver logs are UTC, of
mbt-exec.log local (UTC+2).

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

When the plugin is killed in a pool Prepare after it made a region, the
restarted plugin kept the region for kubelet's Prepare again of the
claim (bug-11: "its Unprepare releases them, its Prepare again reuses
them"). kubelet retries 60-90 s later. Meanwhile the Prepares of other
claims through the same host bridge put their regions on the decoders
above it. If the retry then fails, its rollback destroys the kept
region; if the claim goes before its retry, its Unprepare does. Either
way a region below a newer one is destroyed: a hole in the decoders of
the host bridge and the switch (bug-02), and the kernel creates no
region through them until every region above the hole is gone.

A claim whose device refuses it (Refused, A25: an fs reader of a device
without erofs or without a namespace, a read-only block reader without
a namespace) fails every retry, so after such a cut the hole comes for
sure once anything else is prepared on that host bridge within the
retry interval.

Severity: medium-high. Needs a plugin kill (SIGKILL, OOM, crash, exit
on the loss of NRI) inside a pool Prepare after the region, then a
Prepare of another claim on the same host bridge before kubelet's
retry, then a failing retry or an Unprepare. The effect is severe: no
new region on that host bridge (every pool claim and the local memdev
under it is blocked, "CXL decoders released out of order") for as long
as the newer regions live, possibly the lifetime of a long-running
workload. The driver could avoid it: at start the cut region is the
newest on its ports, and releasing it then is in order.

4. ROOT CAUSE

- D/pool.go:871-917 loadPreparing keeps every claim whose Prepare did
  not finish ("its Unprepare releases them, its Prepare again reuses
  them", :908); D/pool.go:325-327 newPoolDriver does nothing else with
  them; restoreMounts unmounts their node mounts and lease binds only.
- The Prepare again finds the region of the memdev and reuses it
  (M/pmem.go:370-376, M/memctl.go:1015-1019) wherever it lies in the
  decoder stack.
- The release of the kept region comes late: by the rollback of a
  failed Prepare again (D/pool.go:722-751, :524; a refusal of
  D/pool_persistent.go:127, :171, :200) or by the Unprepare of the
  unfinished claim (D/pool.go:1000-1036). By then the Prepares of other
  claims (serialized in p.mu, but kubelet's retry of the cut claim is
  60-90 s away) may have put regions on the decoders above it.
- Linux (bug-02): destroying a region below the last committed decoder
  of a port leaves the decoder counted in decoders_committed; no new
  region through the port until the regions above are destroyed
  (drivers/cxl/core/hdm.c cxl_decoder_reset, region.c
  match_free_decoder).
The model and the contract assumed what the driver should do: faults
neither create nor destroy regions (A9), a refused claim's retry pushes
and pops its region at the top (A25).

========================================================================
5. THE FIX

Release at start, while the cut Prepare's region is the newest on its
ports (nothing ran in between: the plugin is down), and let the
Prepare again make it anew (~0.1-0.3 s more for that Prepare).
a) D/pool.go releaseUnfinished (new :938), called by newPoolDriver
   after restoreMounts and before updateSerials (new :329), so before
   the kubelet plugin registers and before the first scan: for each
   claim whose Prepare did not finish, the release of
   unprepareUnfinished (releaseDevices of unfinishedDevices: node mount
   of the device's erofs, holder wait, offline, disable-region,
   destroy-region, disable-memdev; then deleteRecordedRegions for a
   hollow region of a cut cxl create-region), then forgetPreparing.
   Devices that another prepared claim uses stay as for every release.
b) Kept for the old path (Unprepare releases, Prepare again reuses):
   - companion claims (rec.Lend): the nodes that a numa companion may
     have lent can be revoked only when the lender is set up (after
     NRI, 05-review-DRV3 G4), and the broker learns of the release from
     the Unprepare (OnReleasing);
   - a claim with a region below a region of another memdev already
     (regionsAbove, new :976; a local region made concurrently before
     the kill): releasing it at start would make the hole now;
   - a claim whose release or hollow-region deletion failed (a busy
     node mount, a failed command), or whose record could not be
     forgotten. Its numa node is fenced at start as before.
c) M/decoders.go RegionsAbove (new :81): the regions of other memdevs
   on committed decoders above the memdev's regions in the ports
   between the CXL root and its endpoint (host bridge, switch); ports
   (new :104) and portRegions (new :127) are shared with DecoderHoles.
d) Logs: loadPreparing no longer promises the reuse; releaseUnfinished
   logs "releasing devices ...", "released, its Prepare again prepares
   its devices anew", or why it keeps the claim.
e) Docs: docs/memory/cxl/pool.md (Ordering and failure semantics),
   usage.md (Files on the node: preparingPoolClaims.json).
Not changed: the model and the contract (A9, A25 hold now); the Prepare
again of a kept claim still reuses.
