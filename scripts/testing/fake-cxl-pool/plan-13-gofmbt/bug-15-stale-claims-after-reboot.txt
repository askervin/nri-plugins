BUG-15: PREPARED CLAIMS SURVIVE A NODE REBOOT WITH THE DEVICES OF THE
OLD BOOT

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-29. Found by the reboot research
(12-findings-reboot.txt sections 3 and 4, bugs R1 and R2). Fixed in the
working tree (uncommitted), unit tested, verified on the cluster with the
qemu restart of vm1 (section 7; the guest reboot needs R4 fixed).
Changed after a second review the same day (section 11), not verified on
the cluster again.

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, K/ = ~/github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes
pkg/kubelet/ (v1.37.1-22), RR/ = /tmp/p13-runs/reboot-research/,
B/ = /tmp/p13-runs/b15/ (the verification). Line
numbers of HEAD (73dba0f5a) unless "new". Times of the driver logs are
UTC.

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

After a node reboot kubelet keeps the pods and their claims, loads its
DRA checkpoint with every claim not prepared and calls
NodePrepareResources for each claim before it starts the containers of
the claim (K/cm/dra/claiminfo.go:142-148, K/kuberuntime/
kuberuntime_manager.go:1718-1729: with a new pod sandbox). Both drivers
of the plugin and the lease broker answered from their files with what
the old boot had:
- the pool driver (cxl-pool.nri.io): "already prepared" and the result
  and CDI devices of the old boot: dax device nodes (252:N) that the new
  boot does not have or gives to another device, block device nodes
  (blkext 259:N) that the new boot gives to the next block device, a
  NUMA node without memory, the bind of a node mount that is gone;
- cxl.nri.io: "already prepared" for local slices that the local
  allocator forgot with the state of the old boot, lease claims and the
  companion claims of local leases;
- the lease broker: leases Ready on memory that is gone; the node of a
  numa lease lent again to the restarted requester every minute.
One stale block device node reached another claim's device: after the
qemu restart the container of an rw claim of c-pmem1 read c-pmem0 (its
token mbt-5) through /dev/pmem1 259:0, and could have overwritten the
erofs image that the readers of every VM mount.

R2, part of it and also without a reboot: an fs claim whose node mount
could not be mounted again at start stayed prepared with a CDI bind
mount of the removed directory: every start of its container failed
("failed to fulfil mount request: open .../mounts/c1ae2002-direct: no
such file or directory"), CrashLoopBackOff for ever.

Severity: high. Every reboot of a node with claims (a guest reboot or a
power cycle) gives the containers memory that is not theirs or none: a
container of an rw device can write another device's data, numa claims
run without their memory, fs claims never start, leases lie Ready.

4. ROOT CAUSE

- D/pool.go:472-480 prepareClaim returns pc.Result for any claim in
  p.prepared; D/pool.go:1370-1396 load reads preparedPoolClaims.json
  without a boot id; D/pool.go:1314-1341 buildCDISpec builds the CDI
  devices from the stored dax and block numbers and node mounts. Nothing
  tells the claims of the old boot.
- D/pool_persistent.go:810-816 restoreMounts: a node mount whose device
  check fails (checkBlockDevice: "mem3 has no region") is removed and the
  claim kept prepared with its CDI bind mount (R2).
- D/node_state.go:977-985 prepare: "already prepared" for every claim of
  preparedClaimsInfo.json, although the local allocator dropped the
  state of another boot (D/local.go:1337-1341); D/node_state.go:1105-1117
  forgetGoneSlices drops only the dax device nodes.
- D/lease_store.go:156-178 loadLeaseStore and D/broker.go:133-179
  newBroker: no boot id; the reconcile re-applies the node of every Ready
  numa lease (relendContainer), and NRI Synchronize seeds the restarted
  requester with it (keptLends, LeaseNodes).
- D/pool.go:705-723 checkSamePreparation compares a new claim's device
  with the device records of every prepared claim, those of the old boot
  too; D/driver.go:399 seeds the fence with the nodes of every prepared
  claim (12-findings 4.2: "fencing CXL nodes 2-3").
- Only the local allocator knew the boot (localDevices.json bootID,
  D/local.go:225-228).
- The kernel: a reboot leaves no region, nvdimm region, dax or block
  device, mount or online memory of the old boot; dax (252:N) and blkext
  (259:N) minors are given out anew. The labels of persistent memory
  stay in the LSA (12-findings 3.2, 4.3).

5. THE FIX

Design: every record of a Prepare carries the boot id of its kernel
(/proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id, read once at start). At start a claim
of another boot is not prepared: its record is kept for its Unprepare,
it has no CDI device, and kubelet's Prepare again (which comes before
the containers start) prepares it as a new Prepare. Leases of another
boot are dropped. The pool driver keeps its stale claims as records of
preparingPoolClaims.json, the machinery of bug-11 (no CDI device, not a
user of the device for other claims and releases, Unprepare releases
"as they are now", Prepare again replaces the record), marked Stale so
that the start does not release them (bug-14 releases only what a cut
Prepare left at the top of the decoders; another boot left nothing).
Not chosen: keep stale claims in the prepared map with a flag (every
user of p.prepared, CDI, results, fence, preparedDevice, mountUsed,
regionUsed, leaseBindUsed, restoreMounts, would need the filter); release
stale claims at start (it disables memdevs that kubelet prepares again a
minute later, +2 s probe grace each; the Unprepare of a claim whose pod
went releases its device as the pool expects anyway).

a) Boot id: D/local.go readBootID (:271, its warning no longer says
   "local:") and otherBoot (new :285): a record is of another boot when
   both ids are known and differ. A record without a boot id (written by
   a driver before this fix) is taken as one of this boot and gets the
   id at start: the upgrade of the driver happens without a reboot, and
   taking such claims as stale would leave the running pods without CDI
   devices (kubelet does not prepare them again in the same boot) and
   release slices under running containers (the allocator's reconcile).
   The local allocator keeps its own rule (a state file without a boot
   id is of another boot): it forgets without touching. With the boot id
   of the kernel unknown, every record is of this boot, as before.
   D/driver.go newDriver (new :382) reads it once and passes it to the
   pool driver, the local allocator, cxl.nri.io and the broker.
b) Pool driver, D/pool.go:
   - preparedPoolClaim.BootID (new :276), set by prepareClaim (new :548);
     preparingPoolClaim.BootID and Stale (new :295, :302), set by
     notePreparing (new :868), which clears Stale (the Prepare touches
     the devices now).
   - newPoolDriver (new :375): load, loadPreparing, demoteOtherBoot,
     restoreMounts, releaseUnfinished, updateSerials.
   - demoteOtherBoot (new :1024): the prepared claims of another boot are
     demoted; those without a boot id get this one (saved).
   - demote (new :1058): the claim leaves p.prepared and becomes a stale
     record of preparing: its devices as notePreparing records them
     (untouched, new :240: no memdev, region, dax, namespace, block
     device, node mount, loop device), the NUMA node of the region of a
     numa device now (regionNode: -1 after a reboot, so nothing is
     fenced for it; a region there now is fenced), the lend of a
     companion claim. The region names and lent nodes of the old boot
     are dropped (forgetBoot, new :1001): the new boot names other
     regions so, and a revoke of the nodes would wait for the lender for
     nothing. saveDemoted (new :1088): the preparing file first, then the
     state file (a crash in between leaves the claim in both files, and
     the next start demotes it again; if the preparing file cannot be
     saved, the state file keeps the claim). If only the state file cannot
   be saved, it keeps the claim of the other boot: the forget of a stale
   record that is not prepared again (forgetPreparing, new :903-931)
   saves the state file first, and fails (the record kept, the
   Unprepare fails for kubelet to retry) if it cannot. Otherwise a
   restart in the same boot demoted the claim again into a stale record
   that no Unprepare forgets (review, section 11).
   - loadPreparing (new :955, :962): the record of a Prepare cut in
     another boot is stale ("its Prepare did not finish in another
     boot"), without region names and lent nodes: before, the start
     released it and deleted hollow regions by the names of the old
     boot, which may be another owner's now. A stale record is kept also
     while its memdevs are away (a power cycle before the controller
     attached them again), as a prepared claim is: its Unprepare
     releases the memdev when it is back.
   - releaseUnfinished (new :1124) skips stale records.
   - load (new :1576) does not warn about gone memdevs of stale claims.
   - unprepareUnfinished logs "not prepared any more (...)" for a stale
     claim (new :1273).
   What follows from it: no CDI device for a stale claim (buildCDISpec
   reads p.prepared), no results for NRI (ClaimResults), no fenced node
   at start (numaNodes: results of prepared claims and nodes of records
   with a region now; D/driver.go:409 new, comment), its serial still a
   pool serial (Serials: hidden from the local publisher), its device no
   longer that of a prepared claim (checkSamePreparation, the release of
   another claim, mountUsed, regionUsed, leaseBindUsed), its node mounts
   swept by restoreMounts as those of no claim. Its Unprepare
   (unprepareUnfinished) tells the broker of a companion, revokes
   nothing (no lent nodes), unbinds what the lend names (nothing is
   mounted after a reboot; the directories are removed), releases the
   devices as they are now (unfinishedDevices: current block device and
   node mount; after a reboot: cxl disable-memdev), deletes recorded
   regions that are hollow (none of another boot), forgets the record.
   Its Prepare again is a new Prepare (region, onlining with the fence
   before, dax device, namespace probe, node mount, lease bind, lend),
   writes the new result and CDI spec, and forgets the record; a Prepare
   again that fails before it touches a device (a companion whose lease
   is gone) keeps the record for the Unprepare, one that fails after it
   rolls back the devices of the record too (bug-11).
c) R2, D/pool_persistent.go restoreMounts (new :804, :820),
   restoreNodeMount (new :876), unrestoredMount (new :893), remount (new
   :910); D/pool.go poolDriver.unrestored (new :122), prepareClaim (new
   :526), buildCDISpec (new :1508): a node mount that is not mounted after
   the restore at start (checkBlockDevice: the region is gone, or the
   namespace or block device is another one; or the mount failed) is
   unrestored, in memory: every start checks again. Its claims, but
   companion claims, stay prepared and get no CDI device: a container of
   them fails at its creation on an unresolvable CDI device instead of the
   bind of a missing directory at every start, and the start logs "claim
   ... has no CDI device: its node mount ... is not mounted (...)".
   The Unprepare of a prepared claim (D/pool.go unprepareClaim new :1489,
   D/pool_persistent.go releasedDevices new :416-464) waits for the
   holders of the block device of the region now when the recorded one
   is not the one prepared: its node mount is unrestored, or
   checkBlockDevice fails (for mode block too). The recorded name may be
   another device's now, whose holder it would wait for in vain ("still
   in use" at every retry). Without a region it waits for no block device
   (as unfinishedDevices); when the region is there but its namespaces
   cannot be read, it waits for the recorded one, as a release without
   the check could release a device in use. Logged:
     cxl-pool.nri.io: claim NS/C: device 0x...: mem0 has no region; device 0x... has no region now: its release waits for no holder of /dev/pmem0
     cxl-pool.nri.io: claim NS/C: ...; the release of device 0x... waits for the holders of /dev/pmem1, the block device of its region now, not of /dev/pmem0
   (review, section 11).
   kubelet's Prepare again of such a claim (with a new pod sandbox)
   mounts it again if it can (remount) and rewrites the CDI spec, or
   fails and says why: "device 0x...: its node mount M is not mounted and
   cannot be mounted again: ...; unprepare the claim (delete its pods) to
   prepare it anew". The Prepare of another claim that mounts the node
   mount (preparePersistent, new D/pool_persistent.go:150) gives them
   their CDI devices back. The Unprepare is that of a prepared claim: the
   holder checks, the release, the recorded region deleted when hollow.
   This is also the safety net for a reboot that the boot ids do not tell
   (an older driver's file, an unreadable boot id): the fs claims fail
   their Prepare again clearly instead of starting on nothing. Companion
   claims are not changed (no CDI device; their lease bind is bound again
   only with the node mount, as before).
   Not chosen (the first version, changed after the review of section
   10): demote such a claim as one of another boot. A claim of this boot
   may still have running containers, and its other devices would lose
   the protection of a prepared claim (the releases of other claims,
   regionsAbove); a mount that failed for a passing reason would stay
   failed for the rest of the boot.
d) cxl.nri.io: PreparedClaimInfo.BootID (D/node_state.go new :102),
   set by prepareAllocatedDevices (new :1239); newNodeState (new :193,
   :223) calls forgetOtherBoot (D/prepared_claims.go new :123): the
   claims of another boot are dropped from both maps and the files (their
   slices and lent nodes are gone; the local allocator forgot them),
   those without a boot id get this one. Their Unprepare finds nothing
   (nil), their Prepare again prepares them anew (a new slice, the lease
   socket of a lease claim again, OpenClaim keeps its FsDir). The local
   allocator's reconcile at start takes only the claims of this boot as
   prepared (D/driver.go newLocalHelper new :637).
e) Leases: leaseStore.BootID (D/lease_store.go new :153) and
   forgetOtherBoot (new :200), called by newBroker (D/broker.go new
   :172) before the sockets are created: the leases of another boot are
   dropped and logged ("lease ... is of another boot (...): released,
   its companion is deleted"), the store gets this boot (saved). A table
   that could not be decoded (Recovered) is neither changed nor saved: a
   save would replace it by an empty table, and the next start would
   remove the directories of the lease claims (sweepFsDirs). The
   lease claims stay (kubelet prepares them again). The first reconcile
   deletes the companion pods and claims, which have no lease now
   (deleteOrphans); kubelet then unprepares the companion claims (pool:
   stale record, the device released; local: nothing). A Prepare again
   of a companion before that fails (OnLending: not the companion of a
   lease of the broker). Nothing is lent again: LeaseNodes and the
   reconcile see no lease, so neither NRI Synchronize (keptLends) nor
   relendContainer gives the restarted requester the old node, and
   LendsRestored follows the first reconcile as before. The requester
   lists no leases and asks again. A store without a boot id is of this
   boot.
f) The fence (D/driver.go:409 new): seeded with the nodes of the
   prepared claims of this boot only; the Prepare again of a numa claim
   fences its node before it onlines the memory.
g) Unchanged within a boot: the boot ids are equal, nothing is demoted
   or dropped, results, CDI specs, regions and mounts stay as they were
   at every driver restart (the test90 model relies on it), but the CDI
   devices of the claims of a node mount that the start cannot mount
   again (c; before, they bound a missing directory). The files
   get the field "bootID" (per claim in preparedPoolClaims.json,
   preparingPoolClaims.json and preparedClaimsInfo.json; at the top of
   leases.json); the observer and oracle of test90 decode them
   leniently. leases.json is written at start when its boot id changes
   (also when it did not exist: {"claims":{},"leases":{},"bootID":...}).
h) Docs: docs/memory/cxl/pool.md (Ordering and failure semantics: node
   plugin restart, new item Node reboot), leases.md (Life cycle: Node
   reboot), local.md (What Prepare does), usage.md (Files on the node).

Left (open):
- An older driver's files across a reboot (upgrade during a reboot) are
  taken as this boot's once: R1 then for that boot, but for fs claims
  (no CDI device, their Prepare again fails clearly, c) and dax slices
  of cxl.nri.io (forgetGoneSlices).
- A region on the device of a stale claim at start (firmware-committed
  decoders that Linux assembles, possible on real hardware, not in the
  qemu VMs: the reset uncommits every HDM decoder) is kept for the
  Prepare again, which reuses it; a late release by a failing retry or
  the Unprepare could then leave a decoder hole as in bug-14. Its numa
  node is fenced at start.
- Downgrade: an older driver decodes leases.json strictly and starts with
  an empty lease table on the new field (as for every field added).
- The fs lease of a companion claim whose node mount cannot be restored
  stays Ready without its bind (as before); the broker is not told at
  start.
- A reboot that the boot ids do not tell: the fs claims fail their
  Prepare again clearly (c); the claims of the other modes get the old
  devices as before (the dax slices of cxl.nri.io lose their device
  node, forgetGoneSlices).
- In one boot a block claim whose namespace got other numbers keeps its
  old CDI device node (only fs claims are checked at start).
- While the boot id cannot be read the lease table keeps the boot id
  that it has: leases made meanwhile are dropped at a later start that
  reads another boot id.

9. PROPOSED COMMIT MESSAGE

cxl: do not return claims of an earlier boot as prepared

After a node reboot kubelet prepares every claim again before it
starts the containers. The driver answered with the result of the old
boot: device nodes that the new boot gives to other devices, a NUMA
node without memory, the bind of a node mount that is gone. A
container of an rw claim read another pool device's data.

Record the boot id with the prepared claims of both drivers, with the
records of unfinished pool Prepares and in the lease table. At start a
claim of another boot is not prepared. It has no CDI device and its
nodes are not fenced. kubelet's Prepare again prepares its devices
anew, its Unprepare releases them. The leases of another boot are
dropped, and the reconcile deletes their companions. A pool claim
whose node mount is not mounted again at start gets no CDI device
until it is. Its Unprepare waits for the holders of the block device of
its region now, not of the recorded one. Records without a boot id are
taken as of this boot.
