BUG-19: A PREPARE BEFORE THE NRI PLUGIN IS CONNECTED LOSES THE
STEERING AND THE FENCE OF THE CONTAINERS OF THE CLAIM

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-10-29. Found in a review (the start order checked
by the orchestrator). Fixed in the working tree (uncommitted), unit
tested; untested in the cluster.

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, K/ = ~/github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes
pkg/kubelet/ (v1.37.1-22), NRI/ = github.com/containerd/nri v0.12.2 (the
module of go.mod). Line numbers of HEAD (73dba0f5a) unless "new".

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

The driver registered its kubelet plugins before it started its NRI
plugin. Between the two, kubelet could prepare a claim, and then create
the containers of the pod before the NRI plugin was in the runtime. The
plugin never saw the CreateContainer of such a container; it found the
container only at its Synchronize:
- a container that its claims steer (container.steering, e.g. policy
  auto) got no steering plan at CreateContainer and so no manager at
  StartContainer. Synchronize takes it as not steered by this process
  (syncManagement, releaseSteering), and it runs unsteered for its
  life, memory.high max;
- a fenced container (fencing allowed-mems) got no cpuset.mems
  adjustment at CreateContainer, and ran with the fenced nodes until the
  first fence pass (NRI synchronized and the lends restored).
The window opens at every start of the driver. After a node reboot
kubelet retries the Prepare of every pod of the node every ~10 s while
the driver starts, so the window is likely hit then. After a driver
restart in the same boot it needs a new pod whose Prepare comes then.

Severity: medium. Steering is lost for the life of the container, with
no error; a fenced container uses fenced nodes for a while. No data loss.

========================================================================
2. REPRODUCTION

Not reproduced on the cluster. In general: a claim that steers its
container (or fencing allowed-mems), and a Prepare that kubelet sends
after the kubelet plugin of the driver is registered and before the
NRI plugin has synchronized (a node reboot with such a pod kept, or a
new pod while the driver starts). Unit test: section 6
(TestPrepareWaitsForNRI fails without the wait).

========================================================================
4. ROOT CAUSE

The start order of newDriver: the kubelet plugins before the NRI
plugin, and no Prepare that waits for it. Nothing needed that order:
startNRIPlugin uses the node state, the prepared claims of the pool
driver (loaded by loadPoolDriver) and the lease table (newBroker), all
there before the kubelet plugins start.

========================================================================
5. THE FIX

a) D/driver.go newDriver (new :440-484): the NRI plugin starts after the
   broker is created and before kubeletplugin.Start, and the lenders of
   both drivers are set then too (a companion Prepare right after the
   registration has its lender). The broker starts its reconcile where
   it did, after the ResourceSlice is published (new :522). A failed
   NRI start continues without it as before (fence.setNoNRI).
b) D/driver.go waitNRI (new :702-729) and nriPrepareWait (30 s, well
   within kubelet's 45 s call timeout, K/cm/dra/plugin/
   dra_plugin.go:43): PrepareResourceClaims of cxl.nri.io (new :687) and
   of cxl-pool.nri.io (D/pool.go new :514, before p.mu, so that the wait
   does not hold up the Unprepares) wait until the NRI plugin has
   synchronized (WaitSynchronized), at most nriPrepareWait. On a timeout
   every claim of the call gets the error, nothing is touched, and
   kubelet retries the Prepare:
     NRI not synchronized yet: context deadline exceeded: the containers of the claims would be created before the NRI plugin sees them; kubelet retries the Prepare
   and the driver logs it as a warning ("cxl.nri.io: preparing N claims:
   ..."). No wait without an NRI plugin (it failed to start). The pool
   driver gets the plugin as poolDriver.nri (D/pool.go new :88).
   With a), the wait ends at once in practice: the plugin is in the
   runtime long before kubelet has found the registration socket. It is
   kept because stub.Start returns before Synchronize, and it makes the
   order a guarantee.
c) A Prepare of a claim that is prepared already waits too, as a new
   one: kubelet creates the containers of the pod after any successful
   Prepare (in the same boot kubelet does not prepare a claim again that
   its checkpoint has as prepared, so after a driver restart nothing
   waits for these; after a reboot that the boot ids do not tell, the
   claims answer "already prepared" and their containers are created
   then). Its cost is nil once NRI has synchronized.
d) Not waited for: the lends restored (the broker's first reconcile,
   which needs the API server). A new container gets the kept lends of
   its leases at CreateContainer (keptLends), and a fenced one its
   cpuset.mems without the fenced nodes then; neither needs the
   reconcile.
Not changed: the driver restart in the same boot (kubelet does not
prepare the prepared claims again; the running containers are found by
Synchronize as before), Unprepare (no container is created after it).
Left: a container created between the end of our Synchronize handler
and the runtime adding the plugin is impossible (the runtime holds
syncLock across both); one created while NRI is down (the plugin lost
its connection and the process exits to be restarted) is found by the
Synchronize of the next process, as before.

8. PROPOSED COMMIT MESSAGE

cxl: start NRI before the kubelet plugins, Prepare waits for it

The driver registered its kubelet plugins before it started its NRI
plugin. kubelet could prepare a claim in between and create its
containers before the NRI plugin was in the runtime. The plugin never
saw their CreateContainer: a container that its claims steer ran
unsteered for its life, a fenced one ran with the fenced nodes until the
first fence pass. After a node reboot kubelet retries every Prepare
while the driver starts, so this is likely then.

Start the NRI plugin and set the lenders before the kubelet plugins. A
Prepare of either driver waits until the NRI plugin has synchronized, at
most 30 s, and fails for kubelet to retry otherwise. Without an NRI
plugin it does not wait.
