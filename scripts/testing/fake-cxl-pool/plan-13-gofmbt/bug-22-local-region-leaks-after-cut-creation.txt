BUG-22: THE REGION OF A LOCAL MEMDEV LEAKS WHEN THE PLUGIN STOPS
DURING ITS CREATION, AND THE MEMDEV IS NEVER PUBLISHED AGAIN

Branch 5jg-cxl, 2026-11-02. Found by run FR2b of the model-based test
(15-runs-fencing-reboot.txt, FR2b; step 299 settle failed after 3 min:
"pod lnuma@vm0#0: phase Pending ready false (ContainerCreating)").
Predicted as E3 in 01-findings-driver.txt section 4 and never fixed.
Fixed in the working tree (uncommitted), unit tested, verified on the
cluster by the replay that failed before the fix (section 7).

D/ = cmd/plugins/dra-driver-cxl/, M/ = pkg/cxl/memctl/,
R/ = /tmp/p13-runs/FR2b/, B/ = /tmp/p13-runs/b22/. Line numbers of HEAD
(366ccf18c) unless "new". Times of the driver logs and the kubelet
journal are UTC, of mbt-exec.log and mbt-cxl.log local (UTC+2).

========================================================================
1. SUMMARY

The local allocator records a new local device in localDevices.json
with Region "" before it creates the devdax region of the memdev's
first claim, and again with the region after the region is created and
probed. When the plugin process stops in between, the region stays on
the memdev and the state has the device without it. The start forgot
such a device "without touching the memdev". The publisher then never
published the memdev again ("it has regions [region2] that the plugin
did not create"), and kubelet's Prepare retries of the claim failed for
ever with "could not find allocatable device memdev-c100e2e1". Every new
local claim of the node was Unschedulable until the region was destroyed
by hand or the node rebooted.

A graceful driver restart (SIGTERM) is enough: run() returns right
after driver.Shutdown, and the gRPC server of the kubelet plugin stops
without waiting for a running Prepare, which is cut wherever it is,
also in its rollback. A driver kill, a crash and a containerd restart
cut it the same way. The window is the creation of the region of the
first claim on a memdev without a region, about 0.9 s on the cluster
(region, kmem settle, device_dax bind, address line probe); for a shared
local device it includes the zeroing of the whole device.

Severity: high for the node: the local memdev is lost until a manual
cleanup or a reboot. Needs a driver stop during the first local Prepare
of a memdev. A driver-restart right after a create of an lnuma pod hit
it at the first try (FR2b and its replay).

4. ROOT CAUSE

- D/local.go:587-606 device(): the new device is saved with Region ""
  (:596) before createProbedRegion (:598, region and probe,
  D/local_probe.go:249-275); the second save (:601) comes after it.
  D/local.go:494-517 sharedDevice() does the same (:504, :517), with
  the zeroing of the whole device (zeroShared, :514) inside the window.
- D/local.go:612-618 checkRegions: the record is made only for a memdev
  without a region. So every region on the memdev of a record with
  Region "" is what the creation left. D/local.go:636-640 (createRegion)
  draws that conclusion for a failure in the running process; the start
  did not.
- D/local.go:1205-1209 reconcileDevice: a record with Region "" was
  forgotten "without touching the memdev".
- D/local.go:1140-1150 releaseRegion did nothing to the memdev of a
  record with Region "", so the leftovers branches of sharedDevice
  (:481-486) and device() (:573-578) could not clean it either.
- D/node_state.go:599 (and :652 for a shared device): a memdev with a
  region that the allocator does not know is not published.
- D/main.go:306-313 and D/driver.go:861 (helper.Stop, the gRPC server's
  Stop): a running Prepare is cut, also in its rollback (rollbackContext
  survives the cancel but not the process exit). A SIGTERM cancels the
  Prepare's context first: the creation fails, and its rollback
  (dropUnused) destroys the region in the old process if it gets that
  far.
Decoder order (bug-02, M/decoders.go): when a region of another memdev
lies above the leftover region in the decoders of the host bridge (a
pool Prepare on the same host bridge that ran concurrently), releasing
the leftover leaves a hole, and no region goes through those ports until
the regions above are gone. The rollback of a failed local Prepare had
the same problem (bug-21 section 8 "Left": dropUnused, D/local.go:1154).

5. THE FIX

Design: a record with Region "" means "the plugin was creating a region
on a memdev that had none", and since the review it names the region
that cxl create-region creates (Creating). What the creation left is
released when that is in order, and kept for the claim of the cut
Prepare when a region of another memdev lies above it (bug-14's rule for
the pool driver, memctl.RegionsAbove): the claim holds the region, its
Unprepare releases it (the hole of an Unprepare is the order the user
chose, A9), its Prepare again sets it up as a new region and slices it.
The same rule for the rollback of a failed Prepare in the running
process. Line numbers "new" are of the working tree after the review
(section 10).

a) The claim is recorded before the region is created (new
   D/local.go:647 device(), :531 sharedDevice()): the first save has
   Claims {uid: null}. A claim without slices holds the region (doc of
   localDevice.Claims, new :116-122). The Acquire that fails deletes
   the entry again unless the region is kept for it.
b) The name of the region is recorded too (review, finding 4): region()
   (new :747) creates it with memctl CreateRegionNoted and regionNote
   (new :771), which saves Creating (new :104-107) before cxl
   create-region runs, and the created name if that differs.
   createdRegions (new :1437) is what a creation left: the region named
   Creating if the memdev has it, else none; for a record without the
   name (an older plugin) every region on the memdev (the memdev had
   none, checkRegions). deleteHollowCreated (new :1469) deletes the
   region named Creating if a cut cxl create-region left it hollow
   (memctl HollowRegion, DeleteHollowRegion). adoptCreated (new :1452)
   takes the one region that a creation left as the region of a record
   with claims, not set up (Region = it, Dax "").
c) Start (Reconcile, new :1542, reconcileDevice :1559, reconcileCreating
   new :1628), for a record with Region "": the creation left nothing
   (no region, none hollow): forgotten (as before, no command); the
   serial is a pool serial now (Reconcile's new pool argument, the
   poolSerials of newLocalHelper, D/driver.go:663): forgotten without
   touching its regions; one region and a claim in the record: adopted,
   then released in order or kept for the claim (releaseOrKeep, new
   :1278); else what the creation left is released (releaseRegion) and
   the record forgotten. For a record with a region, the claims that are
   not prepared lose their slices as before (those whose release fails
   go to orphanOwner, and the claim still counts, finding 2); when no
   prepared claim or orphan holds the region any more, it is kept for
   them if a region of another memdev lies above it, else released
   (before: released always, also below newer regions).
d) keepRegion (new :1508): the claims get entries without slices, the
   dax devices that belong to no slice are deleted (numa ones offlined:
   no memory of a kept region stays online), the region counts as not
   set up (Dax "", finding 1), a warning names the regions above.
e) Dax "" with a region marks a region that is not set up (its creation
   failed or was cut, or it was kept; doc new :109-111). device() (new
   :613, through reuse, new :676) and sharedDevice() (new :473) set such
   a region up again while a region of another memdev lies above it,
   else release it in order and create a new one. setUpRegion (new
   :796): enable-region if a cut release disabled it (no dax region; new
   memctl EnableRegion, M/memctl.go new :1167), else spanKept (new :835:
   delete the dax devices that the cut left, daxN.0 over the whole
   region, bound to device_dax), then memctl CreateRegionSize reuses the
   region (waits for the dax device, offlines kmem memory, binds
   device_dax), and the address line probe runs again. A shared device is
   zeroed again (setUpShared, new :561). A kept region whose memory
   aliases is not created again smaller below newer regions
   (errAliasedBelow, D/local_probe.go new :251, also in
   createProbedRegion new :261): the Prepare fails until nothing lies
   above it.
f) releaseRegion (new :1382) of a record with Region "" releases what
   the creation left (b); the leftovers branches of device() and
   sharedDevice() use it.
g) Releases in order (review, finding 3): releaseInOrder (new :1394)
   uses the new memctl ReleaseRegionInOrder (M/memctl.go new :1218): no
   release while a region of another memdev lies above, and a second
   check after cxl disable-region with regionCreateMu held across it and
   cxl destroy-region, so that a region that a pool Prepare creates in
   between keeps the region (disabled; setUpRegion enables it again).
   Used by releaseOrKeep, reuse, sharedDevice and the alias release of
   createProbedRegion.
h) The rollback of a failed Prepare: dropUnused (new :1484) and
   nodeState.releaseLocal (D/node_state.go new :1058, the new
   localAllocator.ReleaseFailed, new :1195) keep the region of a memdev
   whose last claim this is when a region of another memdev lies above
   it (releaseOrKeep). Release (the Unprepare) is unchanged.
i) nodeState.unprepare (D/node_state.go new :907-915): the Unprepare of
   a claim that is not prepared releases what the local allocator holds
   for it (a kept region); nothing to release is a no-op as before, and
   since the review returns without waiting for a.mu when the snapshot
   does not list the claim (listed, new :1233, finding 5).
j) Docs: docs/memory/cxl/local.md (What Prepare does: the claim and the
   region name recorded before the region, the failed Prepare's kept
   region; Restart: the cut creation, the decoder order rule, the check
   after cxl disable-region, the setup of a kept region).
Decided against:
- Saving the region right after cxl create-region, before the probe:
  it narrows the window but cannot close it (cxl create-region itself,
  SIGKILL). It would also need a mark for "not set up / not zeroed",
  which Dax "" now is. The review added the name before cxl
  create-region instead (b), in its own field: Region stays "" until the
  region is set up.
- A bounded wait of Shutdown for running Prepares: it helps only the
  graceful restart (kill, crash, containerd-restart and OOM still cut),
  delays every restart, and the order of the stops (NRI first,
  D/driver.go:844-863) would have to change. The start must handle a cut
  anyway, and now does.
Not changed: the model and the contract (section 6, "Model").

8. PROPOSED COMMIT MESSAGE

cxl: release or keep the local region of a cut creation

The local allocator saves a new device without its region before it
creates the region of the first claim of a local memdev. When the
plugin stopped during the creation, the start forgot the device but not
the region. The memdev was then never published again, and kubelet's
retries of the claim failed with "could not find allocatable device".
A driver restart is enough: it does not wait for a running Prepare.

The device is now saved with its claim and with the name of the region
before cxl create-region runs, and the start releases that region, or
deletes it if the cut left it hollow. When a region of another memory
device lies above it in the decoders, the region is kept for the claim
instead: its release would leave a hole, and the kernel would make no
region through those ports until the regions above are gone. The
Unprepare of the claim releases a kept region, its Prepare again sets
the region up as a new one: enabled again, its dax device over all of
it, probed, and zeroed for a shared device. The rollback of a failed
Prepare keeps such a region too. A region of the creation is not
released on a memdev that is a pool device now.

A release in order checks again after cxl disable-region, with the lock
of the region creations held until cxl destroy-region: a region that a
pool Prepare creates meanwhile keeps the local region. The Unprepare of
a claim that the local allocator does not know does not wait for the
Prepares of other claims.

Add memctl EnableRegion and ReleaseRegionInOrder.
