Findings
--------
F1 (resolved) D/cxl.mk listed pkg/cxl/cdihelpers/*.go and
   pkg/cxl/discovery/*.go after S1 deleted them, which broke "make cxl"
   and the image build (S1 R-O1). It now lists pkg/cxl/api, device and
   memorypolicy; the make target resolves.
F2 Both drivers skip opaque configs of their driver with an unknown kind
   (parseConfig "continue"s on another kind): a misspelled
   "kind: DeviceConfg" is ignored without an error, and the device gets
   the defaults. The documents say what is refused (unknown fields,
   another apiVersion) and do not claim unknown kinds are refused. For the
   review: an error for unknown kinds of the driver would catch typos.
F3 A DeviceConfig on a cxl-region or cxl-dram request is ignored (only
   memdevs parse it): "mode: dax" on a region gives numa memory without an
   error. Documented ("regions and DRAM are always numa").
F4 errSteered applies to node lends only: a steered container (with a
   numa claim of cxl.generic) can take dax leases. 02 and the old docs
   said "steered or borrowing, never both"; the documents now say "no numa
   lease for a steered container".
F5 "Leases follow the pod object: a finished but undeleted pod keeps its
   leases until its lease claim is unprepared" is carried over from the
   old POOL.md; S1 did not change that logic (requesterDone/containerGone
   act when NRI forgets the container), so it was not re-verified on a VM.
