1. Architecture (orchestrator)
------------------------------
Matches the contract (32) and the ownership split of 31 R2:
- The bind of an fs device is the lend, done by the pool driver inside
  the companion's Prepare (bindLeaseMounts before companionLender.lend,
  before OnPrepared marks the lease Ready), recorded in the lendRecord
  (FsDir, Name, Mounts) that is persisted with the prepared claim, and
  undone by the pool driver inside the companion's Unprepare
  (releasing -> revoke -> unbindLeaseMounts -> releaseDevices). An
  EBUSY of the unbind returns before releaseDevices: the node mount and
  the region stay, kubelet retries. No new reference counting: the
  companion is a prepared pool claim, so mountUsed and preparedDevice
  already count it; the Holder check stays the safety net.
- The broker owns the directories (lease-fs/<claim uid>, created at
  OpenClaim with the socket directory's fs mount point, labelled best
  effort), the name (default the lease id, unique per claim) and the
  second CDI mount [rbind, ro, rslave]. Directory removal is os.Remove
  of entries that have no mount at or below them, never RemoveAll under
  lease-fs (31 R3); removed at CloseClaim, after the last lease of a
  closed claim is dropped (release()), and by a sweep at start and in
  every reconcile.
- The bind is a plain MS_BIND of the node mount (31 R1, probe P4) and
  the result is verified in mountinfo (erofs, the node mount's Source,
  flags ro, nosuid, nodev); a bind without them is unmounted again (E4).
  Existing mounts at the target are adopted only when they pass the same
  check (E3 otherwise, neither used nor unmounted), as the node mounts
  are (22 section 10 R1).
- Restart: restoreLeaseMounts re-binds missing binds of prepared
  companions when their node mount is mounted, then unmounts every
  mount under the lease-fs root that no prepared claim's Lend.Mounts
  lists (a crash between the bind and the state write would otherwise
  hold the superblock forever). D-7 covers the same hole without a
  restart for a failed Prepare whose unbind got EBUSY.
- Validation: V1-V12 in api.Request.Validate, shared by the CLI (exit 2)
  and the plugin; B1-B4 in the broker before anything is stored; the
  texts are those of 32. A shared persistent device keeps mode fs
  (serialOptions sets dax for volatile shared devices only); the
  companion of an fs lease asks the pool only, class
  cxl-pool-persistent, DeviceConfig {fs, readOnly, access}, no path.
- Lock discipline unchanged: hooks touch only the lease table; the
  OnPrepared mismatch path releases b.mu before startRelease, as
  OnLendFailed does. removeFsDir and sweepFsDirs read mountinfo under
  b.mu (one file read).
D's Requests D-1 to D-7 are accepted: none changes a name, text or path
of 32; D-3 (a default name never collides with an explicit one), D-6
(stacked binds) and D-7 (stale binds of a failed Prepare retried at the
next release) close gaps of the contract.

3. Findings of the independent reviewer (Opus, production code only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
No bug in normal operation, no deadlock, no harmful race; validation,
CDI device and mountinfo matching as the contract says. Every finding
needs an abnormal condition. Accepted by the orchestrator, sent to D
with tests (section 5):
R1  pool_persistent.go bindLeaseMount, the found case: an existing bind
    at <FsDir>/<Name> is adopted without checking whether another
    prepared claim lists the target (a lease dropped by the broker while
    its companion is still prepared: a force-deleted companion during an
    EBUSY Unprepare, or a store decode failure followed by deleteOrphans;
    then a new lease with the same --name). Two lendRecords then list
    one target and one Unprepare unmounts the other lease's bind. Fix:
    refuse the adoption when another prepared claim lists the target;
    unbindLeaseMounts skips targets another prepared claim lists.
R2  restoreLeaseMounts: a stale bind whose unmount fails (EBUSY) is
    logged and left in no record: it holds the superblock, waitNoHolder
    fails on every later Unprepare of the serial, only a restart helps.
    Fix: add it to p.staleBinds so that unbindStale retries it.
R3  bindLeaseMount: a new bind whose check fails (E4, or a Mounted error)
    and whose unmount then fails stays mounted and unrecorded (never in
    rec.Mounts). Fix: add it to p.staleBinds.
R4  A failed Prepare whose bind is busy: the bind goes to staleBinds, the
    rollback unmounts the node mount, waitNoHolder fails (correct), the
    serial is in no claim; later unbindStale removes the bind, but
    nothing releases the region: the memdev is unremovable until some
    claim of the serial is prepared and unprepared on the node (the
    existing rollback limitation of usage.md, made likelier). Fix:
    staleBinds carry the serial and block device; unbindStale, after a
    bind is gone, releases a serial that no prepared or inflight claim
    has when nothing holds its block device.
R5  Linux rmdir of a directory that is a mount point in another mount
    namespace succeeds and detaches those mounts lazily (3.18+):
    os.RemoveAll(leases/<uid>) removes leases/<uid>/fs, the mount point
    of every container's /run/cxl/fs; removeFsDir and removeMountDir
    remove lease-fs mount points. A container namespace that outlives
    its lease claim (nsenter, a leaked process, unshare -m) loses its
    copy lazily and the host umount succeeds where it should have been
    EBUSY; only the O_EXCL Holder check then stops the region release
    (verified by the reviewer: it does). Fix: remove the known entries
    of leases/<uid> with os.Remove instead of RemoveAll; a comment at
    waitNoHolder that the Holder check is load-bearing for leases.
S1  A store that fails to decode starts empty, and the start sweep then
    removes the empty lease-fs/<uid> of live lease claims: their
    containers keep an unlinked directory and never see later binds.
    Fix: no sweep at start when the store could not be decoded.
S2  OnPrepared's mismatch text is reachable in one direction only: a
    device of mode fs for a lease of another mode fails earlier with E1
    "its lease claim has no filesystem directory" (OnLending gives no
    FsDir to a non-fs lease), which misleads. Fix: E1 says that the lease
    is not in mode fs when rec.Name is "".
S3  unbindLeaseMounts (D-6) unmounts whatever is topmost at the target up
    to 8 times, a foreign mount stacked on the bind included. Fix: stop
    with an error when the topmost mount is not an erofs lease bind.
S4  isLeaseBind ignores the root field of mountinfo: a bind of a
    subdirectory of the same erofs would pass. Fix: parse Root and
    require the node mount's root.
Unit-test gaps named by the reviewer: the OnPrepared mode mismatch; the
adoption of a bind that another prepared claim lists (R1); the restart
sweep with a busy unmount and a later release of the serial (R2); E4 with
a failing unmount (R3); a failed Prepare with a busy bind and no later
claim of the serial (R4); removeFsDir when Mounts() fails (the directory
must stay); a Prepare past OnLending racing CloseClaim's removeFsDir; a
foreign mount stacked on a bind (S3).

5. Fix round
------------
Sent to D on 2026-10-22: R1-R5, S1-S4 and the tests above. D closed all
nine (34-dynamic-bind-mount-status-D.txt "Fix round"): staleBind records
{Target, Serial, BlockDevice} retried by unbindStale at the start of
every releaseDevices, with the serial released (releaseUnused: no
prepared or inflight claim, holder wait) once its binds are gone; the
restart sweep and a failed E4 unmount feed them (bindOrigin derives the
serial from the node mount with the same source); adoption of a bind
another prepared claim lists refused and never unmounted (leaseBindUsed
with exceptUID); non-erofs mounts on a bind stop the unbind; Root in
mountInfo; leaseStore.Recovered disables the sweeps of the process;
CloseClaim removes cxl.sock, lease-fs/<uid> first and leases/<uid> after
it. New tests: TestPoolLeaseBindChecks, TestPoolLeaseStaleBinds,
TestBrokerPreparedModeMismatch, TestBrokerRemoveFsDirMountsError,
TestBrokerRecoveredStoreKeepsDirs, TestBrokerFsLeaseCloseRace.
Orchestrator read of the fix round: as described; one refinement made in
place: removeClaimDirs removes leases/<uid> with os.RemoveAll once
lease-fs/<uid> is gone (nothing is mounted below it in the plugin's
namespace; before, a stray file a container wrote into /run/cxl kept the
directory forever), leases.md adjusted. go vet, go test -race, gofmt and
golangci-lint of the three packages: clean, 0 issues. Final image:
