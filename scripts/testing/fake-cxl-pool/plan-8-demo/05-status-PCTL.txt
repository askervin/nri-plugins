Exclusive rule (exclusiveResult)
--------------------------------
For an allocation result of a published shared device:
  exclusive = consumedCapacity has no "shares" entry
              OR consumedCapacity.shares >= device.SharesUnlimited
Non-shared and unknown devices are never exclusive. The scheduler fills
consumedCapacity of a multi-allocatable device with the full value when
a claim requests no shares (allocator consumable_capacity.go:127-150), so
"no request" arrives as shares 1M; the missing case is treated the same
way (no shares consumed means no request: exclusive). An exclusive want
is attached with AttachRequest.Exclusive and its status data has
"exclusive": true; a share (shares: "1") has no "exclusive" key
(omitempty, false). The controller does not refuse shares below 1: that
guard is the drivers' (plan-9 D2, DRV).
