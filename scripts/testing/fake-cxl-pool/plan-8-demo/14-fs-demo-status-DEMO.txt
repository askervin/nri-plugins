  memdev size = ram/size + pmem/size (a persistent memdev has ram 0).
  pmem regions get "namespaces": [{name, mode, size, block, major, minor,
  fs}] from the nd region of the same resource (/sys/bus/nd/devices/
  regionN; the block device of the namespace or of its holder pfnX.Y;
  phase 2 fix, the path of 12 C5 does not exist), fs from the 4 bytes at
  1024 of /dev/pmemK (erofs, none, ?). With pmem block devices:
  "mounts" (erofs mounts of /proc/self/mountinfo on /dev/pmemK or on a
  loop device backed by it: target, source, block, loop, dax, kind
  node|lease|other, claimUID and name for lease binds) and "openers" (pod
  processes with an fd of /dev/pmemK, perms rw|ro from fdinfo flags).
  mappers() shares pod_pids() with openers().
