Demo bugs found in the first --auto run and fixed, reviewed here:
- probe.namespaces: the nd region of a CXL pmem region is found by its
  resource (physical address) under /sys/bus/nd/devices, not under
  regionN/pmem_regionN; the block device of an fsdax namespace is on its
  holder pfnX.Y; namespace mode "memory" is fsdax. Matches the kernel's
  layout; the fake tree of selftest models it, with a non-matching nd
  region.
- common.fsdax_data_offset: the fsdax namespace starts with the pfn info
  block (signature "NVDIMM_PFN_INFO" at 4096), and the block device data
  starts at its dataoff: byte 56 of struct nd_pfn_sb (signature 16, uuid
  16, parent_uuid 16, flags 4, version_major 2, version_minor 2, then
  dataoff le64), 2 MiB here. T6, zoom fs and zoom bytes read from there
  (R15; 12 C6 assumed 0, wrongly).
- T5 takes pmemN from block udev events only (the cxl subsystem has a
  mem0/pmem0 nvdimm device).
- T1 result lines wrap to at most 3 rows (R14): the result texts of F3,
  F7 and F8 are longer than 47 columns; pool L7's second line is back.
