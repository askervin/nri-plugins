1. Decisions and deviations from the design
-------------------------------------------
- R4 has --shared: "cxl-request release --all && cxl-request request
  --serial S --shared --mode fs --read-write --name data". Without
  --shared a lease takes the whole device (all 1M shares, leases.md
  "Without --shared the lease takes the whole device"), and reader-c's
  share could not be allocated next to it: R7 ("vm1's reader at once")
  would not happen. The writer's exclusiveness is the attachment's
  (ReadWriteOnce: singleHost in its claim status), not the shares': T2
  says "fs rw (vm1 only)" from singleHost (phase B; was "excl"), T5 "vm1 writer asks fs rw share"
  (what it asks), T3 "writer on vm1 only: writer rw".
- R8 command: kubectl exec writer -- /demo/fswrite --append "A second
  line, $(date +%T)"; kubectl exec reader-c -- cat /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt
  (the design leaves the text open; a longer one makes T1 11 rows).
- R7 and R8 captions (58, 52 and 78 columns for R9) wrap into two lines.
  The selftest's caption rule becomes "at most two lines" (was "at most
  50 columns", which the exact captions of section 3 break). R7's result
  is two rows (Step rows=2): its caption (2) and command (4) leave no
  room for a third.
- mkfs --type writes the whole image (510 MiB) through the fd (finding
  V4: measured 0.3-0.6 s for 510 MiB on the host and in the demo image,
  docker on the host, a file as fd 3), then compares the superblock on
  the device with the image's; ext4 is made with -b 4096 (DAX), every
  type with label "demo". The tool keeps the fd DEMO_HOLD (3) s as
  /demo/mkfs of pool-shared-fs does (T4 "open: writer rw").
- Waiting with a reason (T2 "waiting (Conflict)", T3 "waiting
  (Conflict): ..."): the AttachFailed condition lasts only until the
  scheduler deallocates the claim (dra-claim-condition-watch says so), so
  a 1 s view misses it. The reason comes from the condition of an
  allocated claim, else from the claim's last AttachFailed Event (newer
  than an Attached Event; message "attach fs0 to H: Conflict: ..."). The
  probe gains the kind "events" (namespace of the demo), fetched by t2
  and t3 of pool-fs only.
- pool-fs's T2 and T3 also say "attaching" for an allocated claim of a
  pool device without the Attached condition, and treat an allocated
  claim with AttachFailed as waiting: the scheduler allocates the
  waiting readers again every ~10 s, and lease_memory would show "fs ro
  share" for each such attempt (checked: the current views do, on the
  R7 sample). Kept to pool-fs (the samples of the other cases have no
  claim status); see the request to MOVE.
- T5: a reader's Conflict is shown once ("reader-a: fs0 not on vm2:
  Conflict", from the controller's ERROR line or the AttachFailed Event,
  whichever comes first); the lines of the next attempts of the same
  requester (allocated, waits for the attach, scheduled, FailedScheduling,
  AttachFailed) are left out until it is attached, Ready or deleted, or
  the pool server says "exclusive false" (then the attempts that succeed
  are shown). The controller's "status of <claim>: ...=True reason ...: ..."
  lines are left out everywhere (they repeat the line before them; today
  "AttachFailed" in them matched the generic error pattern: "ERROR status
  of claim ..." at every attempt). Checked on the trace sample: 3
  attempts per reader give one line each.
- T4 of pool-fs: a "node mount <type> ro|rw (dax|cached loopN)" line per
  node mount under the pmem line (superblock ro/rw from mountinfo), on
  top of MOVE's "(block ro|rw)" and the lease binds "mounted: reader-c
  (dax), writer (dax rw)". Kept to pool-fs (it adds a row per node to
  pool-shared-fs otherwise).
- T6 of pool-fs: the superblock in the backing file, read by the new
  bin/fsinfo.py (no files of ext4, xfs or btrfs are parsed): 'ext4 "demo"
  510M, clean' or ', needs recovery' (the journal is in use: a node has
  it read-write) and "superblock written HH:MM:SS"; 'xfs "demo" 510M';
  'btrfs "demo" 510M, generation N'; "no file system (zeros)";
  "signatures of ext4, xfs" (the driver refuses several).
- The fs0 branch of the setup is a separate elif next to pool-shared-fs's
  (no change of MOVE's branch); PMEM_CASE (pool-shared-fs or pool-fs)
  selects the demo image and the block udev stream; POOL_DEVICE and
  DEMO_FSTYPE go into state.env.

6c. Script 4: pool-fs (fs0 persistent, 512Mi, serial 0xc1ae0001)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Any filesystem, read-write: the writer formats fs0 with ext4 (or xfs,
btrfs: "demo setup pool-fs xfs") through a block lease, then mounts it
read-write through an fs lease and writes hello.txt; the readers ask for
it read-only. A reader on the writer's node shares its superblock at
once; the readers of the other node wait while the writer writes
(ReadWriteOnce): a single-host filesystem written on one host and read on
another corrupts or misleads. When the writer leaves, its node turns the
filesystem read-only and the other node mounts it. The pods use the demo
image (mkfs.ext4, mkfs.xfs, mkfs.btrfs). S = 0xc1ae0001, the writer on
vm1, reader-a and reader-b on vm2, reader-c on vm1; the filesystem is at
/run/cxl/fs/data in the containers.
Trust: setup configures the driver with fsTypes: [erofs, ext4] (the
default is erofs only). ext4, xfs and btrfs are not hardened against
crafted images: a node that mounts them trusts every party that can write
the device raw (a read-write block claim or lease of it, as the writer's
in R2, on any node of any cluster that shares the pool, and a read-write
fs claim). erofs alone trusts nobody (docs/memory/cxl/usage.md,
Filesystems). Setup prints this note.
R1 "a writer starts on vm1; it has no CXL memory yet"
   $ kubectl apply -f pool-fs/writer.yaml
   T3 "fs0 @pool 512Mi shared persistent", "no claims". T4 no CXL memory.
   T6 "fs0 512Mi shared persistent free", "attached: nowhere", "no file
   system (zeros)" (setup removed its files; a re-run finds the last
   filesystem).
R2 "the writer asks for the device as a block device"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- cxl-request request --serial S --mode block
   As F2 of pool-shared-fs: T1 "lease L Ready block rw (exclusive)",
   "block /dev/pmem0"; T2 "block rw excl"; T3 "exclusive: writer rw"; T4
   vm1 "mem0 c1ae0001 512M  region0 pmem 512M", "pmem0 510M no file
   system (block rw)"; T6 "attached: vm1 exclusive".
R3 "the writer makes an empty ext4 filesystem on it"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- /demo/mkfs --type ext4
   T1 "wrote an empty ext4 of 510 MiB in 0.4 s". T4 "pmem0 510M ext4
   (block rw)", "open: writer rw" for 3 seconds. T6 'ext4 "demo" 510M,
   clean', "superblock written 12:01:12": read from the backing file on
   the host, 2 MiB into the device (zoom fs).
   Say: mkfs.ext4 makes an image file of the block device's size in the
   container; the tool writes all of it through the fd of the lease (the
   container has no device node).
R4 "the writer mounts it read-write instead"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- sh -c 'cxl-request release --all && cxl-request
     request --serial S --shared --mode fs --read-write --name data'
   T1 "lease L Ready ext4 rw (direct)", "path /run/cxl/fs/data", "attached
   to vm1 only (ReadWriteOnce)". T2 writer "fs rw (vm1 only)". T3 "writer on
   vm1 only: writer rw". T4 vm1 "node mount ext4 rw (dax)", "mounted:
   writer (dax rw)". T5 "vm1 writer asks fs rw share", "writer: fs0 excl:
   rw ext4" (the controller attaches fs0 to vm1 exclusively for a
   read-write ext4), "vm1 node mount pmem0 ext4 rw", "vm1 writer: prepared
   ext4 rw direct", "vm1 writer: mounted data", "vm1 writer: lease Ready
   fs data". T6 "attached: vm1 exclusive", 'ext4 ... needs recovery' (the
   journal is in use while a node has it read-write).
   Say: the lease is one share of fs0, so readers can share it; the
   attachment is the node's alone: no other host gets fs0 while vm1
   writes.
R5 "the writer writes hello.txt"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- /demo/fswrite "Hello from the writer, $(date +%T)"
   T1 'wrote /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt: "Hello from the writer,
   12:01:30"'. T6 unchanged: the view reads the superblock only, never
   the files of ext4 (zoom fs).
R6 "three readers start: two on vm2, one on vm1"
   $ kubectl apply -f pool-fs/readers.yaml
R7 "readers ask to read: vm1's reader at once, vm2's must wait"
   $ for r in reader-a reader-b reader-c; do kubectl exec $r -- cxl-request
     request --serial S --shared --mode fs --name data --wait 0s; done
   T1 "reader-c Ready on vm1, next to the writer", "reader-a reader-b
   waiting: Conflict (N s)". T2 reader-c "fs ro share", reader-a and
   reader-b "waiting (Conflict)". T3 "shared: reader-c ro", "waiting
   (Conflict): reader-a reader-b". T4 vm1 "mounted: reader-c (dax),
   writer (dax rw)" (one superblock: reader-c's bind is read-only), vm2
   no CXL memory. T5 "reader-a: fs0 not on vm2: Conflict" once per
   reader: the scheduler allocates their claims again every few seconds
   and the attach of vm2 fails each time; the trace shows it once. T6
   "attached: vm1 exclusive".
   Pause here: nothing reaches vm2 while vm1 writes. Zooms "claim
   reader-a" (no allocation, or the AttachFailed condition during an
   attempt), "pod reader-a".
R8 "the writer adds a line; vm1's reader sees it at once"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- /demo/fswrite --append "A second line,
     $(date +%T)"; kubectl exec reader-c -- cat /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt
   T1 'reader-c reads it at once: "A second line, 12:02:10"'.
   Say: one node, one superblock: the reader sees what the writer wrote,
   through a read-only bind.
R9 "the writer is done: vm1 turns the filesystem read-only, vm2's readers mount it"
   $ kubectl exec writer -- cxl-request release --all
   T1 "released: L Released", "readers' leases Ready after N s", "vm1:
   ext4 read-only, pmem0 read-only". T2 three "fs ro share". T3 "shared:
   reader-a ro, reader-b ro, reader-c ro". T4 both nodes "pmem0 510M ext4
   (block ro)", "node mount ext4 ro (dax)"; vm2 "mounted: reader-a
   reader-b (dax)". T5 "vm1 writer: unmounted data", "vm1 node mount now
   read-only", "vm1 pmem0 read-only", "fs0 on vm1: not exclusive", the
   attach to vm2, "vm2 pmem0 read-only", "vm2 node mount pmem0 ext4 ro",
   "vm2 reader-a: lease Ready fs data". T6 "attached: vm1, vm2", 'ext4 ...
   clean' and the time of the remount.
   Say: the writer's release synced the ext4 and made it read-only on
   vm1 (block device too) before the controller let another host in;
   vm2 reads what vm1 wrote.
R10 "each reader reads hello.txt; a write fails"
   $ for r in reader-a reader-b reader-c; do kubectl exec $r -- cat
     /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt; done; kubectl exec reader-a -- sh -c
     'echo x >> /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt'
   T1 "3 readers read the same 2 lines", "sh: can't create
   /run/cxl/fs/data/hello.txt: Read-only file system" (the step expects
   the failure).
R11 "everything goes away"
   $ kubectl delete -f pool-fs/
   T2 empty. T3 "no claims". T4 both "no CXL memory". T6 "attached:
   nowhere", free, 'ext4 "demo" 510M, clean' stays for the next run.
xfs: as ext4 (direct, dax), T6 'xfs "demo" 510M'. btrfs: no DAX, so
access cached: T4 "node mount btrfs rw (cached loop0)", "mounted:
reader-c (cached), writer (cached rw)", T5 "node mount loop0 btrfs rw",
T6 'btrfs "demo" 510M, generation N' (each commit increments it).
